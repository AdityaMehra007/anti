## 3. HOW TO BREAK IN: TACTICAL PROTOCOLS

### Protocol A: Visual Polish & "Quiet Luxury"
* **Men:**
  * Tailored linen or Supima cotton shirts (white, sky blue, sage).
  * Bespoke chinos or trousers (navy, charcoal, stone); no loud logos.
  * Leather Belgian loafers, monk straps, or minimalist white leather sneakers (Common Projects / Zegna style).
  * Watch: Classic proportions (36mm–40mm); steel sports or dress watch on leather.
* **Women:**
  * Tailored silk shirts, linen trousers, structured blazer dresses, or understated Indian handloom sarees (Chanderi, Raw Silk, Tussar).
  * Minimalist fine jewelry; delicate gold chains or solitaires.
  * Classic leather totes or crossbodies with clean lines (Celine, Loewe, Mulberry); avoid oversized monogram logos.
  * Polished, understated grooming and manicures.

### Protocol B: Organic Networking Mechanics
1. **The Solo Visit:** Visit Café Noir or Smoke House Deli between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM on weekdays. Carry a quality book, architectural journal, or notebook. Avoid constantly staring at a phone.
2. **The Natural Opening:** Comment on a shared context—such as the art piece on display, the seasonal menu item, or a recent gallery preview. Keep interactions brief, warm, and poised.
3. **The Iron Law of Discretion:** Never ask *"What do you do?"* or *"How much did that cost?"* Ask instead: *"What are you working on that's exciting this quarter?"* or *"Are you heading to the Derby this weekend?"*
4. **Follow-Through:** Exchange contacts discreetly via WhatsApp or a personal business card. Follow up with a short, polite note within 24 hours.

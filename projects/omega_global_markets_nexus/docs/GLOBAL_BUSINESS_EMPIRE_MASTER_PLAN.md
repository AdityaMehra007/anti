## 3. The 5-Year Capital Allocation Master Framework

An elite business compounds capital via the **Rule of Sovereign Reinvestment**:
$$\text{Compounded Enterprise Value} = \text{FCF}_0 \times (1 + g)^5 + \sum_{t=1}^5 \left[ \text{FCF}_t \times \left( r_{\text{reinvest}} \cdot \text{ROIC} + r_{\text{M\&A}} \cdot \text{IRR} + r_{\text{buyback}} \cdot \frac{1}{P/E} \right) \right]$$

### Capital Allocation Hierarchy:
1. **Tier-1: High-ROIC Organic Reinvestment (40%)**:
   * Capacity expansion, high-bandwidth interconnects, proprietary lithography lines.
   * Hurdle rate: $\ge 25\%$ ROIC.
2. **Tier-2: Opportunistic Strategic M&A (25%)**:
   * Bolt-on tuck-in acquisitions that expand the platform's proprietary patent fortress or acquire key shipping lane concessions.
   * Hurdle rate: $\ge 18\%$ IRR.
3. **Tier-3: Anti-Dilutive Share Buybacks & Dividends (35%)**:
   * Systematic buybacks when market price trades below intrinsic DCF value.
   * Retires equity float, driving EPS growth faster than top-line revenue.

## HOW TO APPLY EFFECTIVELY

1. **For Corporate Portals (Puma, Marriott, LVMH, Diageo, IHCL):**
   * Filter specifically for location: **Bengaluru** or **Bangalore**.
   * Search role keywords: `Client Relations`, `Events`, `Guest Experience`, `Community`, `Brand Specialist`, `Marketing`.
2. **For Boutique Studios (DNA Networks, Poonam Sharma, Dinaz, Swordfish):**
   * Send a direct, professional introductory email with your CV and a tailored cover note highlighting your interpersonal skills, passion for event production, and aesthetic sense.
3. **For Freshers (The Leela Palace):**
   * Apply directly to the Leela Leadership Development Programme at **lldp@theleela.com** with your CV, a professional headshot, and a brief introductory video.

## 8. HOW A FRESHER GETS HIRED OVER EXPERIENCED CANDIDATES

When applying to luxury and high-society firms, traditional resumes with grades and technical certifications take a back seat. Hiring directors look for:

1. **Poised Visual Presentation:** Professional attire, immaculate grooming, clean styling, and natural eye contact.
2. **High Social Fluency:** Confident, polite, and articulate verbal communication in English. An ability to hold conversations without displaying nervousness.
3. **Genuine Appreciation for Luxury:** Demonstrating familiarity with brands, horology, fine dining, or hospitality details during interviews.
4. **Discretion & Professionalism:** Showing that you understand confidentiality and will represent the brand with dignity around high-profile clientele.

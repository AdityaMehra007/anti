## 2. THE TOP 5 ROLES WITH DAILY FINE DINING & LAVISH WORK ENVIRONMENTS

In these roles, **eating gourmet food, walking through palatial ballrooms, and hosting dignitaries is your literal job description**.

---

### ROLE 1: Director of Sovereign Protocols & Diplomatic Banquets
* **Primary Workplace:** **The Leela Palace Bengaluru** or **Taj West End**
* **The Daily Lifestyle:**
  * You arrive through grand stone driveways lined with palm trees and water fountains.
  * Your daily routine involves tasting menus with Michelin-starred and master chefs at **Le Cirque Signature** (truffle risotto, lobster carpaccio) or **Jamavar** (dum pukht biryanis, royal dal).
  * You coordinate state banquets, royal weddings for industrial dynasties, and private dinners for visiting heads of state and tech billionaires.
* **Compensation:** **₹28 Lakhs – ₹48 Lakhs / year** + executive palace suite privileges, complimentary fine dining, laundry, and chauffeur allowances.

---

### ROLE 2: High-Society Private Member Concierge & Fine-Dining Curator
* **Primary Workplace:** **The Quorum Club** or **The Collection VIP Suites at UB City**
* **The Daily Lifestyle:**
  * You work from an upscale private club lounge overlooking Cubbon Park and the UB City piazza.
  * Your breakfast is freshly baked croissants and espresso at **Café Noir**; lunch is duck confit or wood-fired European fare at **Sunny's** or **Toscano**.
  * You spend afternoons curating private tasting tables, pairing rare vintage champagnes and aged single malts with visiting founders, venture partners, and artists.
* **Compensation:** **₹22 Lakhs – ₹38 Lakhs / year** + unlimited private dining budgets and event access.

---

### ROLE 3: Private Family Office Lifestyle Director (The Billionaire "Chief of Life")
* **Primary Workplace:** Private residences in **Sadashivanagar**, **Koramangala 3rd Block**, or **Epsilon Villas**
* **The Daily Lifestyle:**
  * You manage the private estate and daily lifestyle for a billionaire family (e.g., tech founders or industrial heirs).
  * You coordinate with private chefs, arrange private air charters to Europe or the Maldives, and oversee fine dining for high-stakes home dinner parties.
  * You dine wherever the family dines—sampling dishes at private farm-to-table venues like **Farmlore** or private dining rooms at **The Oberoi**.
* **Compensation:** **₹35 Lakhs – ₹75 Lakhs / year** + discretionary bonuses and luxury perks.

---

### ROLE 4: Ultra-Luxury Wedding Creative Director & Palatial Experience Producer
* **Primary Workplace:** **Palace Grounds (Bangalore Palace)**, **Taj West End lawns**, and luxury vineyard estates
* **The Daily Lifestyle:**
  * You design multi-day celebrations with budgets between ₹10 Crore and ₹30 Crore.
  * Your work week involves food tastings with royal catering houses (sampling saffron-infused royal kebabs, Belgian chocolate dessert bars, and artisanal cocktails).
  * You manage floral architecture, lighting, and celebrity performers amid grand palace arches.
* **Compensation:** 10%–15% planning retainer; **₹40 Lakhs to ₹1.2 Crore+ per wedding season**.

---

### ROLE 5: Luxury Reserve Ambassador & Sommelier (Diageo Reserve / Moët Hennessy)
* **Primary Workplace:** UB Tower (Vittal Mallya Road), five-star hotel lounges, and private golf clubs
* **The Daily Lifestyle:**
  * You don't sit at a desk. You host curated private dinners at **The Library Bar**, **Farmlore**, and **Highland Nectar**.
  * Every working day revolves around gourmet pairings: oysters with vintage Dom Pérignon, Wagyu beef with Grand Cru vintages, and bespoke desserts with rare single malts.
  * You interact with high-profile guests, private collectors, and foreign dignitaries.
* **Compensation:** **₹24 Lakhs – ₹45 Lakhs / year** + extensive expense accounts and international trips to France and Scotland.

- **Zero Guaranteed Income Claims:** We state unit economics, historical precedents, and conversion probabilities. Never promise passive income.

- **Tamper-Evident Ledger:** Every agent action is recorded in SQLite `audit_logs` with timestamps, caller identity, and action tier.
- **Founder Leverage Protection:** If founder hours exceed 35 hrs/week, non-essential exploration is halted immediately and repetitive workflows are automated.

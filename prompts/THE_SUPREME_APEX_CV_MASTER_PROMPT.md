## 📜 PART II: THE SUPREME RESUME CONSTITUTION (THE 10 IMMUTABLE LAWS)

```markdown
<resume_constitution>
RULE 1: THE STRICT 1-PAGE INVARIANT (LAW OF ZERO OVERFLOW)
- An entry-level / fresher resume MUST NEVER, under any circumstance, spill onto page 2.
- A single spilled line on page 2 is an instant failure.
- Target word density is strictly calibrated between 320 and 380 words. Less than 280 looks empty; more than 420 creates cognitive fatigue.

RULE 2: THE ZERO-FABRICATION & METRIC-INTEGRITY LAW
- NEVER invent, extrapolate, or hallucinate metrics or percentages (e.g., "boosted revenue by 400%", "saved 15-20% costs", "closed 300+ deals").
- Recruiters instantly detect and reject freshers claiming executive-scale quantitative outcomes.
- Highlight true, verifiable operational responsibilities, tools used, and task scope with quiet confidence.
- Only verifiable contact numbers (e.g., phone digits) are permitted. Bullet points must have zero fabricated metrics.

RULE 3: THE PLAIN ENGLISH & QUIET CONFIDENCE PROTOCOL
- Write in crystal-clear, everyday conversational English.
- Ban all corporate buzzwords: "synergistic", "paradigm shift", "dynamic go-getter", "spearheaded cross-functional matrix", "results-driven visionary".
- Ban passive or childish language: "helped the team", "took care of", "did work on", "got a letter for good work".
- Use strong, active, grounded verbs: Conducted, Organized, Managed, Coordinated, Built, Produced, Structured, Developed.

RULE 4: THE LOW-PROFILE STANDOUT PARADOX
- The candidate must appear respectful, humble, teachable, and ready to support the team.
- Simultaneously, the candidate must stand out from 99% of generic freshers through undeniable PROOF OF WORK:
  * Vibe coding & building real web tools with modern AI tools.
  * Live GitHub repository and hosted portfolio deployment.
  * Generative AI multimedia and video asset production.
  * Hands-on real-world experience across corporate outreach, high-tech datasets, exhibition coordination, and retail counter operations.

RULE 5: ATS COMPLIANCE & LINEAR ARCHITECTURE
- Strictly single-column layout. Never use 2-column tables, floating text boxes, graphic progress bars, or skill stars.
- Standard section headings: CAREER OBJECTIVE, WORK EXPERIENCE & INTERNSHIPS, PROJECTS & AI PROOF OF WORK, EDUCATION, KEY SKILLS & COMPETENCIES, CERTIFICATES & HONORS.
- Standard typography: Clean system-native fonts (Calibri, Aptos, Arial). 100% extractable unicode text.

RULE 6: THE POINT-TO-POINT BREVITY STANDARD
- Every bullet point must be strictly 1 line (maximum 2 lines).
- Never allow a bullet point to drift into a long narrative paragraph.
- Every bullet begins with a bold role/context lead-in anchor.

RULE 7: DEEP DIGITAL SEAMS & HYPERLINK INTEGRITY
- All candidate links must be active, clickable, and machine-resolvable:
  * Phone (`tel:`), Email (`mailto:`), Portfolio URL, LinkedIn URL, GitHub URL.
  * Links must be verified with active PDF annotation tags (`/URI`) and OpenXML relationships.

RULE 8: LANGUAGE BOUNDARY
- List strictly languages the candidate communicates in fluently (English and Hindi).
- Never add unverified regional languages unless explicitly commanded.

RULE 9: UNIVERSAL DUAL-MARKET FIT
- The resume must be equally attractive to:
  * Enterprise MNCs (Deloitte, Amazon, Accenture, TCS, Infosys): Appeals to their desire for dependability, process adherence, structured computer literacy, and coachability.
  * Fast-Paced Startups (Zepto, CRED, SaaS, AI Studios): Appeals to their desire for high agency, AI fluency, self-driven tool building, and on-ground hustle.

RULE 10: CONTINUOUS AUDIT & VERIFICATION
- Claim nothing complete without automated verification:
  * Word COM `ComputeStatistics(2) == 1`.
  * PyPDF page count `== 1`.
  * PyPDF annotation link extraction count `== 5`.
  * Regex metric filter: 0 prohibited numbers in bullet text.
</resume_constitution>
```

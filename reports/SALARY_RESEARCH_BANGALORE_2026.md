## 4. COMPENSATION STRUCTURE & STATUTORY ANATOMY

Understanding the precise architecture of corporate compensation packages prevents fresh graduates from misinterpreting headline CTCs.

### 4.1 Fixed Salary Structure (Gross A)
| Fixed Component | Typical % Allocation | Purpose & Regulatory Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Salary** | 40% - 50% | Core taxable base used for PF calculation |
| **House Rent Allowance (HRA)** | 40% of Basic (50% in metros under old regime) | Standard living component |
| **Special Allowance / Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP)** | 30% - 40% | Balancing figure, covers internet, books, fuel, meal cards |
| **Employer EPF Contribution** | 12% of Basic | Mandatory social security investment |

### 4.2 Variable Pay, Bonuses & Equity Components (Gross B)
| Variable Component | Typical Quantum Range | Payout Frequency & Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance Linked Incentive (PLI)** | 8% - 20% of Base | Annual / Semi-Annual based on individual & company rating |
| **Sales Quota Commission (B2B Sales)** | 20% - 35% of Base | Monthly / Quarterly uncapped upon target achievement |
| **Shift Differentials** | INR 4,000 - 10,000 / month | Monthly for US/UK hours support |
| **One-Time Joining Bonus** | INR 50,000 - 1,50,000 | Paid in 1st month salary, 1-year clawback agreement |
| **ESOPs / RSUs** | INR 50,000 - 2,00,000 / yr value | 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff (25% per year) |

### 4.3 Mandatory Statutory Deductions in Bangalore (FY 2026-27)
- **Employee EPF (Provident Fund):** `12% of basic salary (matched by employer)`
- **Karnataka Professional Tax (PT):** `INR 200 / month (INR 300 in February)`
- **Income Tax TDS (New Tax Regime):** `Calculated under New Tax Regime (Zero tax up to INR 7.75L with standard deduction of INR 75k)`

## 🔄 THE SELF-REFLECTION & SELF-CORRECTION LOOP

Every agent in the swarm runs a dual-pass evaluation loop:
1. **Pass 1 (Drafting):** The agent generates initial reasoning and outputs based on inputs and tool data.
2. **Pass 2 (Critic Reflection):** A Chief Revenue Officer reflection prompt inspects the output against 3 strict filters:
   * *Filter A (Commercial Feasibility):* Does this offer real economic value to a B2B buyer?
   * *Filter B (Tone & Conciseness):* Is it free of robotic hype, exclamation marks, and generic corporate clichés?
   * *Filter C (Actionability):* Is the next step completely clear and low friction?
3. **Pass 3 (Final Output):** Only polished, battle-tested intelligence is committed to the CRM or shown to the founder.

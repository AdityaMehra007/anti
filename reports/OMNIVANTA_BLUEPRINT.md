## 📦 2. THE TEN CORE PRODUCTS

1. **Omnivanta Control Tower**: Central command center for agents, workflows, models, projects, security, and AI governance.
2. **Omnivanta Agent Cloud**: Create, deploy, and manage specialized AI agents, subagents, memory, and tool permissions.
3. **Omnivanta Workflow Engine**: Convert enterprise business processes into trigger ➔ context ➔ agents ➔ actions ➔ verification.
4. **Omnivanta Context Engine**: Business context layer connecting customers, employees, products, contracts, and tasks.
5. **Omnivanta Data Fabric**: Federated data layer with data catalog, lineage, entity resolution, and knowledge graph.
6. **Omnivanta Integration Fabric**: Universal connector platform supporting MCP, REST, GraphQL, database connectors, and webhooks.
7. **Omnivanta App Engine**: Low-code enterprise application generator integrating data, workflows, agents, and dashboards.
8. **Omnivanta AI Agent Studio**: Enterprise agent builder with evaluation, simulation, and autonomy controls.
9. **Omnivanta AI Control Tower**: Inventory management, risk monitoring, model gateway, and cost tracking.
10. **Omnivanta Autonomous Workforce**: 14 Specialist AI Workers executing operational jobs across IT, HR, Finance, Sales, Marketing, Research, Security, and QA.

# OMNIMONEY OS: 7-Day Cash Flow Sprint (₹20,000 First Rupee Target)

**Mission**: Convert existing Bengaluru data assets, AI tools, and BBA business background into the first paying client within 168 hours.

---

## The Core Math
- **Target**: 1 Paying Client
- **Offer**: AI WhatsApp Inbound Qualification & Automated Calendar Booking
- **Pricing**: ₹15,000 – ₹20,000 setup fee (50% upfront = ₹7,500 – ₹10,000 cash in bank immediately)
- **Target Segment**: Aesthetic clinics, dental specialists, high-ticket gyms in Indiranagar, Koramangala, and HSR Layout.
- **Conversion funnel required**:
  - 40 targeted local business audits
  - 20 personalized WhatsApp messages sent
  - 5 positive replies / conversations
  - 2 demo calls booked
  - 1 closed deal

---

## Day-by-Day Tactical Execution Plan

### Day 1: Asset Assembly & Prototype Build
- [ ] Configure a test WhatsApp Business automated workflow using Make.com / Gallabox / Wati.
- [ ] Script a 3-step conversation flow:
  1. Instant response (<60 seconds): *"Hi! Dr. Rao's clinic here. Are you looking for consultation on skin treatments, hair restoration, or acne care?"*
  2. Qualification: *"Great! Which day and time slot works best for your 15-minute visit?"*
  3. Confirmation: Integrates with Google Calendar and sends location pin automatically.
- [ ] Verify test message delivery to personal phone.

### Day 2: Prospect Identification & Gap Mining
- [ ] Open Meta Ad Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`) and search:
  - `"Dermatologist Bangalore"`
  - `"Dental Clinic Bangalore"`
  - `"Crossfit Bangalore"`
- [ ] Identify 20 businesses actively spending money on ads.
- [ ] Mystery-shop their contact channels: click their Instagram ad / send a WhatsApp inquiry at 7:30 PM.
- [ ] Document the response lag (typically 3 to 12 hours). Take screenshots of the delay.

### Day 3: Loom Video Audit Creation
- [ ] Record 5 personalized 60-second video audits:
  - Screen 1: Their current ad / website.
  - Screen 2: The lag in response time: *"Hey Dr. [Name], noticed your ad for HydraFacial. When someone clicks at 8 PM after work, your front desk is closed. 60% of these patients book with competitors who reply in 5 minutes."*
  - Screen 3: Show your working 60-second WhatsApp demo bot: *"Here is how we automate bookings into your clinic calendar 24/7."*
- [ ] Upload to Loom or Google Drive as an unlisted link.

### Day 4: Direct WhatsApp Outreach Dispatch
- [ ] Send 15 personalized value messages via WhatsApp Business:
  > *"Hi Dr. [Name], quick observation from your Meta ads for [Clinic Name]. I noticed evening patients wait hours for appointment confirmation. I recorded a 45-second screen demo showing how our AI WhatsApp scheduler automatically qualifies patient treatment interest and books slots directly into your calendar. Can I share the 45-second video link with you here?"*
- [ ] Log responses into `OMNIMONEY_RADAR_BENGALURU.html` CRM pipeline.

### Day 5: Follow-Up & Demo Calls
- [ ] Follow up with all non-responders after 24 hours:
  > *"Just wanted to make sure this didn't get buried! Happy to set this up for your front desk for 3 days completely free to show you how many extra patients it captures."*
- [ ] Host 15-minute Google Meet demos with interested clinic owners. Show the live phone screen receiving the automated booking.

### Day 6: The No-Brainer Close
- [ ] Present the offer:
  - **Setup**: ₹20,000 (Complete setup, calendar sync, staff training).
  - **Guarantee**: *"If this doesn't capture at least 5 patient appointments in your first 14 days, you get a 100% refund."*
  - **Terms**: 50% deposit (₹10,000) to start build today, 50% upon go-live.
- [ ] Send Razorpay / UPI payment link.

### Day 7: Onboarding & Deployment
- [ ] Receive ₹10,000 deposit.
- [ ] Deploy the bot with the clinic's treatment menu and FAQ sheet.
- [ ] Connect clinic Google Calendar.
- [ ] First verified client milestone achieved. Move CRM status to `WON`.

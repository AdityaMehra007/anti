## 🛠️ THE 5 VERIFIED PAYOUT CHANNELS

### 1. 💼 Channel 1: Upwork Direct Local Bank Transfer (Fastest & Simplest)
* **How it works:** When Upwork milestones ($350–$1,500) are released, Upwork sends INR directly to any Indian bank account via IMPS/NEFT at competitive exchange rates.
* **Setup Time:** 2 minutes (Enter IFSC code and account number).
* **Payout Schedule:** Instant withdrawal or automated weekly payouts.

### 2. 🌍 Channel 2: Wise (Formerly TransferWise) Multi-Currency Account
* **How it works:** Provides you with local US (Routing/Account #), UK (Sort Code/Account #), and EU (IBAN) bank account details. Clients in NY or London pay locally with zero wire fees. Wise auto-converts to INR and deposits to your Indian bank in 24 hours with an automatic FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate).
* **Best for:** B2B agency retainers ($1,000–$3,500/mo).

### 3. 💳 Channel 3: Stripe / LemonSqueezy (Merchant of Record)
* **How it works:** Embedded in your Gumroad digital products and Google Sheets Micro-SaaS tools. Accepts international credit cards, handles global VAT/sales tax, and auto-settles daily/weekly payouts to India.
* **Best for:** Sheet2Pipeline subscriptions ($29–$79/mo) and Gumroad Playbooks ($47–$197).

### 4. 🇮🇳 Channel 4: Razorpay / Cashfree / UPI (Domestic India)
* **How it works:** For Bangalore startup founders paying ₹35,000–₹75,000 for pitch deck sprints.
* **Settlement:** Instant UPI transfer (GPay/PhonePe) or T+1 day bank settlement.

### 5. 💰 Channel 5: Outlier.ai & Mercor Direct Payouts
* **How it works:** Outlier and Mercor pay weekly via PayPal or direct bank deposit for completed AI evaluation tasks ($30–$50/hr).

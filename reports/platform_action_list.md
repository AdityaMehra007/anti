### 1. Upwork
* **URL:** [https://www.upwork.com](https://www.upwork.com)
* **What It's For:** The largest global freelance marketplace for B2B Lead Generation, Market Research, Virtual Assistance, and Sales Development.
* **Account Type to Create:** `Freelancer` (Individual)
* **Estimated Setup Time:** 35–45 minutes
* **Priority:** **DO TODAY (High Priority)**

#### 🛠️ Step-by-Step Signup Process:
1. Go to `upwork.com/signup` and select **"I'm a freelancer, looking for work"**.
2. Sign up using your professional Google Workspace email (`adi@...` or standard Gmail).
3. Complete profile creation:
   * **Title:** `B2B Lead Generation Specialist | Market Research & Data Enrichment`
   * **Hourly Rate:** Set to `$20.00/hr` initially to win fast contracts and build Job Success Score (JSS).
   * **Overview/Bio:** Focus on tangible business outcomes (verified prospect lists, zero bounce rates, ICP list building using Apollo/Sales Navigator).
4. Add 3–5 portfolio items (sample lead generation spreadsheets with blurred contact info, industry research reports).
5. Pass identity verification (requires Aadhaar / Passport and a quick selfie).
6. Fill out the W-8BEN tax form (Select **Non-US Person**, treaty benefits applicable).

#### ⚠️ Gotchas & Pro-Tips for India:
* **Connects Budget:** You get 10 free Connects; buy a $15 pack (100 Connects) immediately to submit 10–12 targeted proposals on day one.
* **Payment Setup:** Link your **Direct to Local Bank (INR)** via IFSC/SWIFT (Upwork charges $0.99 per withdrawal) or **Payoneer / Wise** for USD retention.
* **TDS & GST:** Provide your Indian PAN card to prevent 20% backup withholding tax. If GST registered, add GSTIN to avoid 18% GST on Upwork platform fees.

### 18. Wise (formerly TransferWise)
* **URL:** [https://wise.com](https://wise.com)
* **What It's For:** Multi-currency receiving accounts (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD) with direct conversion to INR at the mid-market exchange rate and ultra-low fees.
* **Account Type to Create:** `Personal / Freelancer Account` (India)
* **Estimated Setup Time:** 20 minutes + KYC
* **Priority:** **DO TODAY (Crucial)**

#### 🛠️ Exact Setup Steps for India:
1. Register at `wise.com` using your phone number and email.
2. Complete KYC by uploading your **PAN Card** and **Aadhaar/Passport**.
3. Set up **Inward Remittances to Indian Bank**:
   * Add your primary Indian savings/current bank account details (Account Number + IFSC).
4. Set default **RBI Purpose Code**: Select `P0802 - Software consultancy/implementation / Data processing / Business consulting`.
5. Wise automatically generates digital **FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate)** for every payment received, essential for Indian tax filing.

## 2. Defending the "15% Cost Savings" Metric

### The Recruiter Question:
*"How did you achieve a 15% per-event cost savings?"*

### The Bulletproof Answer:
> "Cost savings were achieved primarily through **direct vendor negotiation and long-term supplier relationship building**. By building a trusted network of recurring suppliers across Bangalore (AV technical teams, stage fabricators, print vendors, and transport logistics), I avoided one-off middleman markups.
> 
> For example, on a standard INR 1,00,000 event budget, procuring AV gear and fabrication directly from primary suppliers rather than secondary sub-contractors saved roughly INR 15,000 per event, which I either passed back to the client as budget savings or reinvested to upgrade event production quality."

### 1. PRINCIPLE OF CONTINGENCY (ZERO UPFRONT COST)
1.1 **Zero Financial Risk:** The Service Provider agrees to perform a retrospective vendor contract SLA audit or cloud/inference infrastructure cost audit at **INR 0 (Zero Indian Rupees) upfront fee**.  
1.2 **Sole Compensation:** Service Provider’s sole financial compensation shall be an agreed percentage of **Actual Net Realized Savings or Recovered Credits** ("Success Fee") achieved by Client resulting directly from Service Provider’s audit findings and recommendations.

### 2. AUDIT SCOPE & METHODOLOGY
2.1 **Audit Subjects:** The audit shall examine:
   - External IT/BPO vendor timesheets vs. actual system delivery logs (Jira, GitHub, CI/CD) over the past 12 months.
   - Contractual SLA penalty clauses (turnaround times, defect rates, service outages, staffing roll-offs).
   - Cloud compute and AI inference token usage (unoptimized model routing, missing semantic caches, unquantized LLM calls).
2.2 **Timeline:** Service Provider shall complete the preliminary audit within twenty-one (21) business days of data receipt.

### 3. SUCCESS FEE CALCULATION & PAYMENT TERMS
3.1 **Fee Percentage:** Client shall pay Service Provider:
   - **Twenty-Five Percent (25%)** of all cash refunds, vendor credit notes, invoice reductions, or penalty offsets recovered from external vendors.
   - **Thirty Percent (30%)** of verified net reduction in monthly cloud/AI API expenditures over a twelve (12) month period.
3.2 **Verification & Acceptance:** Recovered credits or savings shall be documented in an agreed "Recovery Ledger" signed by both parties prior to invoice issuance.
3.3 **Payment Term:** Invoices issued under this Agreement shall be settled within twenty (20) business days ("Net-20") of Client receiving the cash refund, credit note, or monthly billing reduction.

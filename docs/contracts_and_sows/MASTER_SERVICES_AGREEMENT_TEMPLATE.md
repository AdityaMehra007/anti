### 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES & STATEMENTS OF WORK (SOW)
1.1 The Service Provider shall render specialized technology auditing, AI evaluation, software module engineering, and operational governance services as described in mutually executed Statements of Work ("SOW") executed pursuant to this Agreement.  
1.2 Each SOW shall specify: (a) Project Objectives; (b) Technical Deliverables; (c) Milestones and Schedule; (d) Fees and Payment Terms; and (e) Acceptance Criteria.

### 2. PROCUREMENT BYPASS & SUB-THRESHOLD PURCHASE ORDERS
2.1 **Discretionary Sub-Threshold Orders:** The parties agree that an individual SOW with total consideration less than INR 5,00,000 (Indian Rupees Five Lakhs) may be executed under Client’s departmental discretionary spending authority, requiring only single-quote departmental approval and immediate Purchase Order ("PO") generation.  
2.2 **Pass-Through Billing:** In the event that Client requires billing via an existing empaneled Tier-1 vendor, Client shall facilitate a tripartite pass-through arrangement without modifying the technical milestones herein.

### 3. FEES, TAXES & INVOICING
3.1 **Fees:** Client shall pay Service Provider the fees specified in each SOW.  
3.2 **Taxes & GST:** All domestic fees are subject to Goods and Services Tax (GST) at 18% under SAC Code 998313 / 998314.  
3.3 **Zero-Rated Export / SEZ Supply:**  
   (a) Where Client is a registered SEZ Unit (e.g., Cessna Business Park, Manyata Embassy SEZ), services shall be zero-rated under Section 16 of the IGST Act, 2017, upon Client furnishing the relevant SEZ Endorsement / Form I.  
   (b) Where invoices are billed directly to Client's overseas parent entity in foreign currency (USD/EUR), services shall be zero-rated under the Letter of Undertaking (LUT) framework pursuant to FEMA guidelines.  
3.4 **Payment Terms:** Undisputed invoices shall be paid within fifteen (15) business days ("Net-15") from invoice receipt.

### 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
4.1 **Client IP:** All proprietary data, proprietary source code, internal documents, and confidential business information provided by Client remain the sole property of Client.  
4.2 **Deliverable IP:** Upon receipt of full payment, all custom code, audit reports, and documentation created specifically for Client under an SOW shall vest in Client ("Work for Hire").  
4.3 **Pre-Existing Frameworks:** Service Provider retains exclusive ownership of its pre-existing core software libraries, evaluation algorithms, prompt templates, and audit heuristics. Service Provider grants Client a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use such tools integrated into the Deliverables.

### 5. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA SECURITY
5.1 Each party agrees to hold all Confidential Information of the other party in strict confidence.  
5.2 **Zero Data Retention Guarantee:** For AI evaluation and vendor SLA auditing, Service Provider guarantees that no Client training corpora, proprietary code, or financial records shall be stored on external public LLM servers or retained past the milestone validation period. All processing shall occur within Client’s Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) or an isolated sandbox.

### 6. TERM & TERMINATION
6.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of twelve (12) months and shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless terminated by either party with thirty (30) days' written notice.  
6.2 Either party may terminate an individual SOW immediately upon material breach if such breach remains uncured for fourteen (14) days.

### 7. GOVERNING LAW & ARBITRATION
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.  
7.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this contract shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in Bengaluru, Karnataka, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

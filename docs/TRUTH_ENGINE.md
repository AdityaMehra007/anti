# 🛡️ OMEGA TRUTH ENGINE SPECIFICATION

## Core Directive
The Truth Engine rejects ungrounded operational claims and prevents simulations from appearing as external live events.

## Evidence Hierarchy
* **LEVEL 1:** Observed real payment transaction / bank credit.
* **LEVEL 2:** Verified customer production usage.
* **LEVEL 3:** Real customer statement / interview transcript.
* **LEVEL 4:** Existing market demand & commercial filings.
* **LEVEL 5:** Competitor traction & public filings.
* **LEVEL 6:** Search & public market signals.
* **LEVEL 7:** Expert opinion.
* **LEVEL 8:** AI-generated hypothesis.

Level 8 evidence is never promoted to Level 1 without cryptographic external references.

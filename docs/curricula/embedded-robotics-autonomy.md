# University Curriculum Roadmap: Robotics & Autonomous Systems Engineer

> **Role Archetype**: `embedded-robotics-autonomy`  
> **Overview**: Construct real-time robotic controllers, SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping), motion planning algorithms, and embedded microkernel firmware.  
> **Total Curated Courses**: 16 courses across 4 progressive tiers.

---

## Tier 1: Computational Mathematics & Programming
**Target Level**: `Introductory (L1)`  
**Key Competencies**:
- Rigid body transformations (SO3, SE3)
- Calculus for kinematics
- C++ for robotics

# University Curriculum Roadmap: Computer Vision & Multimodal Perception Engineer

> **Role Archetype**: `computer-vision-multimodal`  
> **Overview**: Develop 3D neural reconstruction systems, multi-view geometry, generative diffusion vision, and real-time visual-inertial odometry.  
> **Total Curated Courses**: 16 courses across 4 progressive tiers.

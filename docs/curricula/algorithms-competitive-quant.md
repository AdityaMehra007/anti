# University Curriculum Roadmap: Algorithms, Theoretical CS & Quantitative Engineer

> **Role Archetype**: `algorithms-competitive-quant`  
> **Overview**: Master algorithmic problem solving, graph theory, randomized algorithms, NP-completeness, and computational mathematics for quant trading and algorithm optimization.  
> **Total Curated Courses**: 16 courses across 4 progressive tiers.

---

## Tier 1: Foundations of Algorithms
**Target Level**: `Introductory (L1)`  
**Key Competencies**:
- Asymptotic notation
- Sorting & searching bounds
- Basic dynamic programming

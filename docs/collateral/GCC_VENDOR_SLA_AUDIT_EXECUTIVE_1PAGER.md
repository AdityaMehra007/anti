### The Problem: Multi-Crore Invisible Leakage
Bengaluru Global Capability Centers (GCCs) contract hundreds of external software engineers, QA testers, and operations associates from Indian IT vendors (TCS, Infosys, Wipro, LTIMindtree, and staffing vendors). Across annual IT vendor spend ranging from **INR 50 Crores to INR 500 Crores ($6M–$60M USD)**, empirical data shows **8% to 15% is lost to unmonitored contractual leakage**:
- **Phantom Hours:** Billable hours logged on timesheets that have zero corresponding commit, pull request, or Jira ticket activity.
- **Uncredited SLA Breaches:** Contractual delivery latency penalties that are never claimed due to lack of middle-office monitoring.
- **Bench Roll-Off Lag:** Unfilled positions billed during vendor rotation transitions.

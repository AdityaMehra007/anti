## 1. THE DAILY 7-POINT NON-NEGOTIABLE CHECKLIST

Print this sheet or keep it pinned on your desktop. Every day you tick all 7 boxes, your debt shrinks, your family is safe, and your victory gets closer.

```
DATE: ____________________  | DAY OF THE WEEK: ____________________

[ ] 1. THE SLEEP DISCIPLINE (05:00 AM – 11:30 AM)
    * Got 6.5 hours of deep, uninterrupted REM sleep in a dark, quiet room.
    * Did not look at screens in bed after 04:45 AM.

[ ] 2. THE 3-INMAIL ATTACK (12:00 PM – 02:00 PM)

    * Checked LinkedIn, Cutshort, and Wellfound inboxes for recruiter responses.

[ ] 3. THE DAYTIME INTERVIEW / DEMO SLOT (02:00 PM – 04:30 PM)
    * Attended a scheduled HR screening call, technical round, or refined demo scripts.
    * Reviewed STAR interview defenses before the call.

[ ] 4. THE FAMILY ORIFLAME SYNC (04:30 PM – 05:00 PM)
    * Had a quick 15-minute phone call with Mother to check daily Group BP.
    * Ensured downline orders are flowing smoothly on automated WhatsApp channels.

[ ] 5. THE PHYSICAL WORKOUT (05:30 PM – 06:30 PM)
    * Completed 45 minutes of strength training or cardio.
    * Drank 1 liter of water post-workout. Cleared mental stress and cortisol.

[ ] 6. THE BRIGHTCHAMPS US SHIFT (07:30 PM – 04:30 AM)
    * Logged into dialer on time; completed 60+ consultative calls to US parents.
    * Handled pricing objections with calm value breakdowns.
    * Drank only water, black coffee, or green tea (zero junk food/sugary drinks).

[ ] 7. THE DEBT & CASH LEDGER CHECK (04:30 AM)
    * Verified that today's daily income target was protected.
    * Reminded myself: "Every call I made tonight is taking down my ₹30L loan."
```

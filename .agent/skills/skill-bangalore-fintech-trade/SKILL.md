# Bengaluru FinTech & Trade Finance Operations Operational Skill

**Skill ID:** SKILL-BLR-FIN-04  
**Category:** Global Trade Finance & Investment Banking Operations  
**Associated Subagent:** `fintech-banking-agent`  
**Candidate Grounding:** Aditya Mehra (BBA International Business 2026, DSU)

## 1. Scope & Objective
Standard operating protocol for Tier-1 Bengaluru investment banks, global wealth managers, foreign institutional banks, and FinTech unicorns (Goldman Sachs Bengaluru, JPMorgan Chase GSC, Morgan Stanley, Standard Chartered GBS, Deutsche Bank CIB, HSBC, BNY Mellon, Razorpay, CRED).

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. **Letter of Credit (LC) Scrutiny under UCP 600:**
   - Audit incoming and outgoing documentary credits against ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600) and ISBP 745.
   - Screen for common non-conformities: late shipment dates, discrepant descriptions of goods between invoice and B/L, port of loading ambiguities, and unauthorized partial shipments.
2. **SWIFT Message Processing & Nostro/Vostro Reconciliation:**
   - Verify SWIFT MT700 (Issue of a Documentary Credit), MT707 (Amendment to an LC), MT799 (Free Format Financial Message), and MT103/MT202 wire payments.
   - Reconcile Nostro and Vostro balances, identifying unallocated debit/credit entries within a strict 4-hour SLA window.
3. **FX Spot/Forward Settlement & AML Compliance:**
   - Cross-verify cross-border currency conversion rates against Bloomberg/Refinitiv terminal benchmark fixes.
   - Execute mandatory OFAC sanctions screening and FATF PEP checks on foreign beneficiary entities prior to fund release.
4. **Outreach & Executive Proposal Protocol:**
   - Target VP of Global Trade Operations, Head of Trade Finance Middle Office, and Director of Treasury Settlements with ready-to-deploy UCP 600 discrepancy audit sheets.

## 3. Defense & Verification Rules
- 100% middle/back-office banking operations, trade finance, and regulatory reconciliation.
- Strict zero-sales boundary: No credit card sales, no insurance pushing.
- Formally defended using BBA International Business trade finance rigor and transaction analysis.

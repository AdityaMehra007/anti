# SKILL: High-Ticket Closer

## Objective
Convert 20–30% of scheduled discovery calls into signed, paid contracts at $3,000–$5,000 upfront + recurring retainers.

## Execution Directives
1. **The Doctor-Patient Frame**:
   - Never pitch in the first 7 minutes.
   - Ask clarifying diagnostic questions to isolate where pipeline leaks and revenue drop-offs occur.
2. **The Contrast Principle**:
   - Contrast the high cost and low reliability of hiring full-time SDRs ($5,000/mo salary + benefits) against an automated, guaranteed AI engine ($3,000 setup).
3. **The Risk Reversal Close**:
   - Eliminate purchase friction with the 15-meeting guarantee: *"If we don't deliver 15 qualified meetings in 45 days, you get a 100% refund or we work for free until you do."*

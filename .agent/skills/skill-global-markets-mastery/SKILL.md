## 📊 1. EQUITIES EVALUATION FRAMEWORK (STOCKS)

### Rule 1: The Three Valuation Pillars
1. **Free Cash Flow Yield:** $\frac{\text{FCF}}{\text{Enterprise Value}} > 5\%$ for value, $> 3\%$ for growth.
2. **Return on Invested Capital (ROIC):** $\text{ROIC} > 15\%$ indicates durable competitive moats.
3. **PEG Ratio (Price/Earnings-to-Growth):** $\text{PEG} < 1.5$ to avoid overpaying for momentum.

### Rule 2: India Equities Specifics (Nifty / NSE / BSE)
* Target secular tailwinds: Infrastructure CapEx, Semiconductor Packaging, Defense Indigenization, Power Grid Modernization.
* Avoid companies with promoter pledging $> 10\%$ or persistent corporate governance red flags.

## 💵 2. MONEY MARKETS & FIXED INCOME (DEBT)

### The Yield Curve Signal (10Y minus 2Y Spread)
* **Inversion ($10\text{Y} < 2\text{Y}$):** Late-cycle warning; credit tightening; prioritize liquidity & high-quality cash.
* **Steepening ($10\text{Y} > 2\text{Y}$ post-cut):** Early-cycle expansion; allocate toward growth equities and commercial expansion.
* **Risk-Free Rate Benchmarks:**
  - US 3-Month T-Bill (Floor for global USD returns).
  - India 91-Day T-Bill & RBI Repo Rate (Floor for risk-free INR capital).

## 💱 3. FOREX & CURRENCY ARBITRAGE (FX)

* **USD/INR Long-Term Deprecation Drift:** Historically ~2.5% to 3.5% annualized INR drift against USD due to inflation differentials.
* **The Structural Dollar Arbitrage:** Earning in USD / GBP / AED while living in Bangalore creates a structural **3x–4x purchasing power advantage**.

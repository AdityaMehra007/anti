# SKILL: Autonomous Outbound Lead Engine

## Objective
Generate a predictable, inbound flow of 15–30 qualified B2B sales calls per month at zero ad spend.

## Execution Directives
1. **Targeting (ICP)**:
   - Industry: B2B SaaS, Marketing/Staffing Agencies, High-Ticket Consultancies.
   - Company Size: 5–50 employees.
   - Decision-Maker: CEO, Founder, Managing Partner, Head of Growth.
2. **Dynamic AI Personalization**:
   - Extract company news or growth signal from LinkedIn / website.
   - Generate a single-sentence observation (<25 words) showing direct understanding.
3. **Multi-Touch Rhythm**:
   - Touch 1 (Day 1): 90-word email focusing on speed-to-lead and the Loom video offer.
   - Touch 2 (Day 3): LinkedIn connection note + question on current pipeline strategy.
   - Touch 3 (Day 6): 2-sentence low-pressure bump offering the workflow architecture diagram.

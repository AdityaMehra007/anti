# SKILL: Content-to-Pipeline

## Objective
Convert technical architecture breakdowns and client results into 5–10 inbound qualified discovery calls per week.

## Execution Directives
1. **The Architecture Teardown Structure**:
   - Hook: Contrast high SDR costs ($90k/yr) vs automated AI pipelines (<$300/mo compute).
   - Core Value: Share a real mermaid flowchart or code snippet showing how the speed-to-lead engine works.
   - Call-to-Action (CTA): *"Comment or DM 'ENGINE' and I'll send over the 1-page architecture blueprint."*
2. **DM Inbound Conversion**:
   - When a prospect comments or DMs, reply with the blueprint link and ask: *"Curious, what does your current inbound lead response time look like right now?"*
   - Transition warm answers to the 15-minute diagnostic calendar link.

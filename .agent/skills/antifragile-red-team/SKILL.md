# Antifragile Red Team Skill

This skill operationalizes OMEGA Constitution Section 14 (Adversarial Probes) and Mode H (Red Team) to guarantee the sovereign enterprise cannot be killed by any single black-swan failure or state-sponsored attack.

---

## 1. The Core Adversarial Axiom

> "A system that merely survives stress is robust. A system that gains strength, captures stranded volume, and expands market share during systemic collapse is antifragile."

The enterprise must treat black-swan shocks as opportunities to swallow brittle competitors:
1. **Grid Shocks**: When the municipal power grid suffers brownouts, our collocated SMR nodes provide islanded 24/7 power, allowing data centers and factories to run while competitors halt.
2. **Connectivity Partitions**: When undersea fiber cables are cut, local 200 Hz edge kernels continue precision manufacturing autonomously, storing state deltas locally until re-synchronization.
3. **Supply Embargoes**: When critical minerals are banned, drop-in magnet-free reluctance motor topologies and 18-month stockpiles ensure unhindered delivery.

## 2. The 4 Mandatory Red-Team Stress Probes

Every quarter, the agent must run the 4 sovereign failure probes:
1. **Probe Alpha (50% Baseload Loss)**: Microgrid islanding must fire in $\le 120\text{ ms}$, shedding batch AI training to maintain 100% robot telemetry and safety control.
2. **Probe Beta (Trans-Oceanic WAN Partition)**: Local edge autonomy must maintain 200 Hz control barrier functions with zero telemetry drift, pausing only high-uncertainty tasks.
3. **Probe Gamma (Critical Mineral Embargo)**: Verify that alternative supply channels or SynRM motor designs can maintain production without NdFeB rare earths.
4. **Probe Delta (Fiat SWIFT Freeze)**: Clear machine-to-machine transactions over private peer-to-peer cryptographic channels in Energy-Compute Units (ECU) with zero dependence on commercial banks.

## 🛠️ STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

### Step 1: Real-Time Domain Inspection & Tech Detection
* Extract `<title>`, `<meta name="description">`, and check JavaScript signatures for:
  - CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive
  - E-Commerce: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento
  - CMS: Webflow, WordPress, Next.js
  - Payments: Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay

### Step 2: Seniority & ICP Scoring (1–100)
* **Founder / CEO / Co-Founder:** +30 points
* **VP / Head of Growth / Sales:** +25 points
* **Sweet-Spot Company Size (10–100 employees):** +10 points
* **Lead Tier:** HOT (80+), WARM (60–79), COLD (<60).

### Step 3: Bespoke 3-Touch Omnichannel Cadence (Sub-80 Words)
* **Touch 1 (Day 1 - Observation & Value):** Specific operational hook + 0% bounce rate verified data offer.
* **Touch 2 (Day 3 - Proof & Short Loom Offer):** 10-lead sample proof + low-friction question.
* **Touch 3 (Day 7 - Polite Permission Break-Up):** Respectful closing of the loop.

---

## 🛑 NON-NEGOTIABLE COMPLIANCE RULES
1. Never use spam trigger words ("free", "guarantee", "buy now", "click here", "100% discount").
2. Always keep cold emails under 80 words.
3. Strictly use low-friction calls to action (*"Open to checking a 3-lead sample?"*).

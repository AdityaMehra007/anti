## 1. Scope & Objective
Standard operating protocol for Tier-1 Bengaluru GCCs (Google India, Microsoft India, Apple India, Amazon Development Center, Target Tech, Walmart Global Tech, Bosch Engineering). Focuses on cross-border program management, vendor master service agreement (MSA) governance, SLA audit controls, and multi-region business continuity.

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. **Global Morning Handover (09:00 - 10:00 IST):**
   - Ingest Jira / ServiceNow tickets logged by North America (US West/East) and EMEA timezones.
   - Categorize severity, identify SLA breache risk, and allocate triage workflows across India operations.
2. **Vendor MSA & Performance Audit:**
   - Audit external vendor deliverable milestones against contractual Statement of Work (SOW) stipulations.
   - Run quarterly business reviews (QBR) tracking variance in vendor defect rates, turnaround time (TAT), and billing accuracy.
3. **Cross-Border Process Automation:**
   - Map redundant human-in-the-loop steps across international clearance pipelines.
   - Build automated data dashboards in Power BI / Tableau tracking real-time regional operational velocity.
4. **Outreach & Positioning Protocol:**
   - Target Directors of Global Operations, VP of Business Transformation, and GCC Site Leads with audit-first portfolios.

## 3. Defense & Verification Rules
- 100% operational excellence and enterprise vendor governance.
- Rooted in real corporate brand operations at Puma & Tata Communications (vendor logistics, on-ground asset reconciliation).
- Output format: SLA reconciliation matrices, vendor scorecards, and incident post-mortem retrospectives.

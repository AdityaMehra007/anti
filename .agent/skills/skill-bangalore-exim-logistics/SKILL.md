# Bengaluru EXIM & Global Logistics Governance Operational Skill

**Skill ID:** SKILL-BLR-SCM-03  
**Category:** Global Supply Chain & International Trade Logistics  
**Associated Subagent:** `scm-exim-agent`  
**Candidate Grounding:** Aditya Mehra (BBA International Business 2026, DSU)

## 1. Scope & Objective
Standard operating protocol for global ocean carriers, air freight forwarders, retail logistics hubs, and aerospace manufacturers in Bengaluru (Maersk GCC, Kuehne+Nagel, DHL Global Forwarding, Walmart Global Tech Supply Chain, Apple India Supply Chain Operations, Boeing India, Bosch Automotive).

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
1. **Trade Documentation & Customs Scrutiny:**
   - Verify Bill of Lading (B/L) / Air Waybill (AWB), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Certificate of Origin against DGFT and Indian Customs regulations.
   - Enforce rigorous Incoterms 2020 risk allocation (FOB vs. CIF vs. DDP/DAP) to eliminate buyer-seller dispute liability.
2. **ICEGATE & Port Clearance Tracking:**
   - Monitor Bill of Entry (BOE) status on ICEGATE portal for Chennai Port and Bengaluru Kempegowda Air Cargo Complex.
   - Flag customs tariff classification disparities under Indian Customs Tariff / HS Code standards to prevent demurrage penalties.
3. **Landed Cost Variance Modeling:**
   - Model end-to-end landed costs: Base FOB price + ocean freight + marine insurance + basic customs duty (BCD) + IGST + inland drayage.
   - Calculate container demurrage and detention thresholds, flagging container turnaround bottlenecks.
4. **Outreach & Portfolio Protocol:**
   - Connect directly with Heads of Logistics, Customs Compliance Managers, and Supply Chain Directors with pre-built landed cost calculators and customs clearance checklists.

# OMEGA Zero-to-One Venture Engine Skill

This skill operationalizes Section 17 of [`OMEGA_CONSTITUTION.md`](../../OMEGA_CONSTITUTION.md) to launch resilient, defensible, high-leverage products and ventures from scratch.

## 1. The 22 Foundations
For every venture or product opportunity, produce:
1. Problem (painful, urgent, high-cost)
2. Customer (specific ICP with budget authority)
3. Current Alternative (workaround currently tolerated)
4. Value Proposition (10x better, faster, or cheaper)
5. Product Concept (core capability)
6. Minimum Viable Product (smallest testable artifact)
7. Business Model (monetization mechanism)
8. Pricing Structure (value-aligned pricing tiers)
9. Distribution Channels (organic, outbound, partnerships)
10. Customer Acquisition Strategy (scalable CAC)
11. Retention Loops (switching costs and compounding utility)
12. Unit Economics (Contribution Margin, LTV/CAC > 3x)
13. Technology Stack (modular, minimal, proven)
14. AI Layer (genuine leverage, not performative wrappers)
15. Automation Systems (eliminating repetitive manual ops)
16. Talent / Role Structure (Build, Buy, Partner, or Automate)
17. Capital Requirements (runway to cash-flow positive)
18. Risk Assessment (regulatory, technological, market)
19. Competitive Response (defense against copycats)
20. Defensible Moat (network effects, data flywheel, switching cost)
21. Expansion Opportunities (adjacent markets)
22. Global Strategy (international scaling playbook)

## 2. Four-Horizon Execution Gates
- **24-Hour Validation**: Smoke test customer interest (landing page, direct outreach, interview feedback).
- **7-Day Experiment**: Functional prototype deployed to 5-10 real test users.
- **30-Day MVP**: Working v1.0 software with end-to-end user flows and telemetry.
- **90-Day Business**: Repeatable sales/distribution motion with positive unit economics.

# STAR INTERVIEW DEFENSE: RAZORPAY BANGALORE

### 1. "How do you handle merchant resistance on transaction fees and MDR?"
* **Direct Framing:**  
  "Selling regulated financial products at Bajaj Allianz and driving ₹60L+ in commercial sales taught me that price is only an issue in the absence of value. When a merchant pushes back on basis points or fees, they are really asking: 'Will this reduce my payment failures, prevent checkout drop-offs, and save my finance team manual reconciliation headaches?' I don't get defensive. I walk them through the conversion uplift: if Razorpay's superior routing and 99.9% uptime saves even 2% of their dropped carts, that 2% revenue recovery completely outweighs any marginal difference in gateway fees. I sell bottom-line reliability, not commoditized basis points."

---

### 2. "Why choose FinTech partnerships when your background has direct selling and events?"
* **Direct Framing:**  
  "Direct selling, live events, and insurance are the ultimate masters degrees in understanding human financial motivation and logistics. In events, I had to manage cash flows and vendor disbursements under intense deadlines. In insurance, I had to pass IRDAI exams and explain legal fine print to customers. When you look at Razorpay's merchant suite—payments, vendor payouts, corporate cards, and tax payments—it is solving the exact operational friction I lived every single day. I know what a business owner cares about because I've run businesses."

---

### 3. "How do you manage high quotas without burning out?"
* **Direct Framing:**  
  "By running systems, not relying on motivation. At BrightCHAMPS, I make 60+ outbound calls a night during US hours. At Oriflame, I maintained 7,500+ Group BP month after month for years. I don't rely on sporadic bursts of effort; I maintain a strict daily routine, organize my CRM pipeline methodically, qualify fast, and follow up with structured cadence. Discipline beats burnout every single time."

## 5. STAR Interview & Business Challenge Defense Guide

### Challenge 1: "How do you evaluate whether to source a component locally in India vs. importing from East Asia?"
* **Framework:** Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis.
* **Key Factors to Articulate:**
  1. *Landed Cost:* Ex-factory unit price + freight (air/ocean) + import tariffs/customs duties + insurance.
  2. *Lead Time & Working Capital:* Sourcing from overseas may have a 35-day transit time requiring higher safety stock holding costs vs. 4-day local turnaround.
  3. *Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ):* Evaluating cash-flow lockup for large foreign shipments.
  4. *Quality & Defect Returns:* Local suppliers allow faster inspection and defective unit replacement compared to complex cross-border return logistics.

### Challenge 2: "A shipment of critical parts is stuck at customs in Chennai due to a documentation discrepancy. What do you do?"
* **Action Steps:**
  1. Immediately verify the discrepancy on the Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, and Packing List (e.g., mismatch in HS Code, declared valuation, or country-of-origin certificate).
  2. Contact the Customs Broker / Freight Forwarder to obtain the formal query notice.
  3. Coordinate with the supplier to issue a revised, stamped amendment letter or corrected document within 2 hours.
  4. Alert the production planner so factory scheduling can adjust assembly queues to prevent worker downtime while the clearance is resolved.

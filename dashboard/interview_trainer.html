Q2. Operations / Cost Savings Defense
"How exactly did you achieve your 15% average per-event cost savings?"

By guaranteeing volume to primary suppliers and eliminating middleman markups, I achieved an average 15% net savings per budget."
✔ Key Defense Points: Primary vendor relationships, eliminating middleman markups, volume commitments.
Q3. Business Development / Lead Generation

✔ Key Defense Points: 15+ concurrent threads, lead qualification, management commendation.
Q4. International Business / Technical Knowledge
"In global trade, what is the difference between FOB and CIF in Incoterms 2020?"
"Under FOB (Free On Board), seller risk and cost transfer once goods are loaded on board the vessel at port of shipment; buyer pays ocean freight and insurance. Under CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), the seller pays freight and mandatory marine insurance up to the destination port, though buyer assumes cargo loss risk after loading."
✔ Key Defense Points: Incoterms 2020, risk transfer point, ocean freight & marine insurance responsibility.

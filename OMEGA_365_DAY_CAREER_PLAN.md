# OMEGA 365-DAY CAREER EXECUTION & RADAR PLAN

**Objective**: Systematic, evidence-grounded execution for maximizing interview conversion and career ROI over 365 days.  
**Candidate Alignment**: BBA International Business $\rightarrow$ Global Supply Chain / Trade / GCC Operations.  

---

## 1. 365-Day Operational Loop Architecture

```
================================================================================
                    365-DAY CONTINUOUS EXECUTION CADENCE
================================================================================

DAILY (Morning Loop):
• Discover & verify new requisitions across 20 Tier S/A/B Bangalore companies
• Audit existing job availability; detect removals/changes
• Recalculate Expected Career Value (ECV) and generate ATS evaluations
• Prepare tailored application dossiers & recruiter InMail drafts
• Human review and gated dispatch

WEEKLY (Strategy & Funnel Audit):
• Calculate conversion rates: Discovered -> Verified -> Submitted -> Replies -> Interviews
• Review outreach response ROI by company tier and message template
• Adjust keyword weights and resume positioning based on verified reply telemetry

MONTHLY (Career Milestone Review):
• Benchmark compensation offers against Bengaluru GCC market curves
• Assess emerging skill demands in AI-enabled operations and global trade
• Rebalance company tier watchlists based on hiring velocity signals

QUARTERLY (Strategic Reset):
• Deep multi-model strategy debate on market trajectory
• Archive expired opportunities and refresh network intelligence graph
================================================================================
```

## 3. Human Approval Gates (Strict Zero-Delusion Policy)

The autonomous system executes discovery, verification, scoring, resume customization, and dossier preparation.
**Human Approval is strictly required before**:
1. Sending external recruiter emails or InMails.
2. Submitting applications on official company career portals.
3. Placing business telephone calls or scheduling calendar invites.
4. Committing financial transactions or contract acceptances.

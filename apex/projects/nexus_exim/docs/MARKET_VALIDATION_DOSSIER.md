## 2. Real Verified Pain Points (Ranked by Severity)

1. **Document Discrepancies (The #1 Hold Cause):** Inconsistencies between commercial invoices, packing lists, and ICEGATE shipping bills cause Let Export Order (LEO) holds and query issuance by customs appraisers.
2. **Demurrage & Detention Accumulation:** When containers remain at ICD Whitefield or port terminals (Chennai/JNPT) past standard free-time (7–14 days), penalties range from ₹5,000 to ₹25,000/day.
3. **HSN Classification & Duty Surcharges:** Basic Customs Duty (40% BCD on capital/solar goods), 10% Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS), and 18% Integrated GST (IGST) require precise pre-calculation to avoid penalty notices from DGFT/CBIC.
4. **Regulatory Multi-Agency Burden:** Simultaneous compliance across CBIC (Customs), DGFT (EPCG/Advance Auth), BIS/QCO certifications, and RBI (eBRC remittance closure).

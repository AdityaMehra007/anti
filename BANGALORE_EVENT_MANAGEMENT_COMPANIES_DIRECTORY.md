## 6. KEY ROLES IN EVENT MANAGEMENT & HOW TO BREAK IN

### A. Core High-Value Roles
1. **Show Director / Festival Producer:** Managing technical stage crews, sound engineering, artist timings, and live show flow.
2. **VIP Hospitality & Artist Relations Lead:** Handling celebrity hospitality riders, airport tarmac escorts, five-star hotel suites, and backstage catering for international headliners.
3. **Wedding Creative Director & Set Scenographer:** Conceptualizing themes, mood boards, floral pavilions, and lighting design.
4. **Corporate Experiential Account Director:** Pitching and winning multi-crore budgets from Google, IBM, or Cisco for their annual summits.

### B. Starting Out as a Fresher
* **Entry Roles:** *Hospitality Coordinator, Artist Shadow, Production Runner, Decor Assistant.*
* **Starting Compensation:** **₹3.5L to ₹7L / year** + all-expense-covered travel across music tours, luxury resorts, and palace banquets.
* **How to Apply:** Send your profile directly to **DNA Entertainment Networks**, **Toast Events**, **Tamarind Global**, or **George P. Johnson (GPJ India)** through their website careers pages or LinkedIn recruiting leads.

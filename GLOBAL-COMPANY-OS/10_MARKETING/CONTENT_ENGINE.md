## 1. Editorial Strategy: The Regulatory Authority Flywheel

Rather than producing generic fluff, the TradeNexus Content Engine focuses on **high-intent, high-urgency programmatic regulatory intelligence**. When a customs regulation changes (e.g. EU CBAM Q3 updates, DGFT SCOMET revisions), exporters urgently search for exact HS-code implications and filing formats.


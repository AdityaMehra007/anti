## 2. Channel Breakdown & Operational Playbooks

### Channel A: Direct Personalized Compliance Teardowns (Primary Beachhead)
- **Target**: Curated lists of 4,500+ Indian exporters (DGFT / ICEGATE registry data).
- **Execution**:
  1. Engine analyzes exporter's public product catalog and recent shipping trends.
  2. Generates a pre-computed compliance gap teardown (e.g. `AUDIT_SUNDRAM_FASTENERS_LIMITED.html`).
  3. Sends personalized email to Managing Director / VP Export Logistics showing exact potential demurrage risks.
  4. Offer: Free live test on 5 shipping bills.
- **Conversion Metric**: Target 15% meeting booking rate; 40% trial-to-paid conversion.

### Channel B: Export Councils & Association Alliances (FIEO / EEPC / ACMA)
- **Partner Entities**:
  - Federation of Indian Export Organisations (FIEO)
  - Engineering Export Promotion Council (EEPC)
  - Automotive Component Manufacturers Association of India (ACMA)
- **Format**: 45-minute technical compliance masterclasses ("Navigating the 2026 EU CBAM & US Tariff Tightening Without Port Fines").
- **Conversion Mechanism**: Attendees receive 30 days of complimentary access to TradeNexus AI for up to 50 dockets.

### Channel C: Customs Broker (CHA) Alliances (Force Multiplier)
- **Positioning**: TradeNexus does not replace the CHA; it empowers the CHA to offer error-free guarantees to their clients.
- **Incentive**: 15% recurring SaaS revenue share for every exporter onboarded through the CHA's partner portal.
- **Outcome**: 1 CHA onboarded unlocks 20–50 exporter accounts simultaneously.

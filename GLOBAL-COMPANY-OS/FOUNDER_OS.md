# FOUNDER EXECUTIVE OS & COGNITIVE LOAD CONTROL

## The Sacred Founder Rules
1. **The Rule of 3**: The founder never receives a list of 50 tasks. The Company Commander presents exactly **Top 3 High-Leverage Actions** every morning.
2. **Founder Focus Protection**:
   - High-Value: Strategy, closing customers, approving capital/legal, reviewing architecture.
   - Delegated to AI: Research, parsing, drafting, testing, competitor tracking, report generation.
   - Eliminated: Vanity social posting, premature administrative meetings, endless feature brainstorming.
3. **Daily Cadence**:
   - **08:30 AM**: Review Daily Command Center Brief (What matters, What changed, What to do).
   - **09:00 AM – 01:00 PM**: Deep Execution Block (Customer conversations, product testing, closing).
   - **02:00 PM – 05:00 PM**: Strategic Review, agent feedback, pipeline optimization.
   - **05:30 PM**: Evening ledger sign-off and shutdown.

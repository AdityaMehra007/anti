## 2. THE LADDER OF DELEGATION (WHEN TO HIRE)

Never hire an employee until the work has climbed all 6 rungs of The Ladder:
1. **Can this task be eliminated?** (YAGNI / Strip bloat).
2. **Can this task be automated with software?** (Python scripts / cron / API).
3. **Can this task be handled by an AI Agent?** (Antigravity 27 Specialist Swarm).
4. **Can this task be handled by an external on-demand contractor?** (Upwork / specialized legal or accounting firm).
5. **Can this task be handled by a commercial partner?** (Customs broker / freight forwarder).
6. **Only then**: Propose a full-time human employee.

# TradeNexus AI: Founder Cognitive Protection & Anti-Busywork Protocols

## 1. Operating Philosophy: The Anti-Busywork Engine

In compliance with **Part LIX (Anti-Busywork Engine)** and **Part LV (Founder Evolution)**:
The founder must not be trapped in low-leverage repetitive administrative tasks. The company must progressively automate operational workflows, freeing the founder to focus solely on high-judgment capital allocation, critical customer relationships, enterprise sales, and strategic architecture.

## 3. The 3 Golden Rules of Cognitive Protection

1. **Rule of Elimination**: Before automating any process, ask: *"Can this workflow be completely eliminated without harming customer value or legal compliance?"* If yes, delete it immediately.
2. **Rule of Async First**: Zero unscheduled synchronous meetings. All customer feedback, bug reports, and agent alerts must be logged in written, structured formats with clear reproduction steps.
3. **Rule of Rulings over Stalls**: In accordance with `AGENTS.md`, minor ambiguities are resolved immediately via the Decision Ledger rather than stalling execution for consensus.

# CUSTOMER MAP & BUYER PERSONA DOSSIER

## Primary ICP: Mid-Market Export Manufacturer
- **Industry**: Precision Engineering, Automotive Components, Industrial Chemicals, Specialty Textiles.
- **Annual Export Turnover**: ₹15 Crore to ₹250 Crore ($2M to $30M).
- **Monthly Shipment Volume**: 5 to 50 export containers/air consignments.
- **Headquarters**: Bengaluru, Peenya Industrial Area, Whitefield, Bommasandra, Hosur corridor.

## Buyer Persona 1: Managing Director / Managing Partner (Economic Buyer)
- **Pain**: Terrified of shipment delays holding up working capital and customer payment releases.
- **Goal**: Expand export margins and avoid compliance penalties under new trade treaties.
- **Trigger**: Received notice of cargo inspection hold or penalty from an overseas port.

## Buyer Persona 2: Export Logistics Manager (User Buyer)
- **Pain**: Drowning in manual paperwork; spends 4 hours per shipment checking HS codes and certificates of origin.
- **Goal**: Fast, error-free documentation that customs brokers accept without revisions.
- **Quote**: "If a single digit is wrong on the shipping bill, the container sits at Chennai port for 5 days."

## 2. Partnership Tiers

### Tier 1: Customs House Agent (CHA) Alliance Program
- **The Problem for CHAs**: Customs brokers operate on razor-thin clerical fees, suffer from high clerk turnover, and face severe client backlash when shipments are stuck at destination ports.
- **The TradeNexus Solution**: We provide the CHA with a "White-Label or Co-Branded Compliance Gateway". The CHA passes invoices through TradeNexus, offering their clients a "100% Zero-Demurrage Guarantee".
- **Commercial Model**: 15% ongoing monthly revenue share on all software subscriptions generated from their client base.

### Tier 2: Trade Finance & Banking Desks
- **Partner Entities**: Exim Bank of India, State Bank of India Trade Finance, HDFC / ICICI Global Trade Desks.
- **The Collaboration**: Banks face heavy clerical costs checking trade documents against Letter of Credit (LC) rules (UCP 600). TradeNexus audits dockets pre-presentation, ensuring clean first-time submissions.
- **Commercial Model**: Enterprise API licensing or per-audit fee.

### Tier 3: ERP & Accounting Platforms
- **Partner Entities**: Tally Solutions, Zoho Books, SAP SME Partner Network.
- **Integration**: Native "TradeNexus Export Compliance" button inside Tally Prime and Zoho Books. An exporter generating an export invoice clicks one button to verify customs compliance before printing.

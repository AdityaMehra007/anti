# TradeNexus AI: Standard Channel Partner Referral Agreement

**THIS CHANNEL PARTNER AGREEMENT** is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between:

1. **TradeNexus AI** (A division of Global Company OS / Parent Entity), having its principal technology operations at Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Company"); and
2. **Channel Partner** (Authorized Customs House Agent / Trade Consultant / Technology Partner) ("Partner").

---

## 1. Appointment & Scope
The Company hereby appoints the Partner as a non-exclusive referral partner to introduce prospective exporter clients ("Qualified Exporters") to the TradeNexus AI autonomous compliance platform.

## 2. Partner Compensation & Referral Fees
- **Referral Fee**: Company shall pay Partner a recurring commission of **15% (fifteen percent)** of Net Software Revenue collected from each Qualified Exporter referred by Partner.
- **Duration of Commission**: Commission applies for the lifetime of the client contract (up to a maximum of 36 consecutive months from initial activation).
- **Payment Terms**: Commission payments are calculated monthly and disbursed within 15 days following the close of each calendar month.

## 3. Partner Obligations
- Partner agrees to introduce TradeNexus AI to its export client roster using official marketing materials and technical demonstration sandboxes provided by Company.
- Partner shall NOT make any unauthorized representations, warranties, or price concessions on behalf of the Company.

## 4. Confidentiality & Data Protection
Both parties acknowledge that in the course of referral activities, sensitive exporter commercial data (shipping bills, product descriptions, pricing) may be reviewed. Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

## 5. Term & Termination
This Agreement remains in effect for an initial term of 1 (one) year and automatically renews annually unless terminated by either party with 30 (thirty) days written notice. Accrued referral commissions on existing active clients shall survive termination.

# 2028–2050 FUTURE SCENARIO ENGINE

## Scenario A (2028): Pervasive Agentic Trade
- **Driver**: Autonomous purchasing bots deployed by global retailers (Walmart, Target, Carrefour) automatically issuing RFQs and selecting exporters based on real-time compliance scores.
- **Our Strategic Adaptation**: Position TradeNexus as the verified API bridge certifying exporters for autonomous procurement systems.

## Scenario B (2032): Radical Geopolitical Tariff Fragmentation
- **Driver**: Escalating carbon border tariffs (CBAM), regional trade pacts, and shifting tariff schedules updated weekly across 150 countries.
- **Our Strategic Adaptation**: Moat expands exponentially. No human broker can track multi-country dynamic tariffs; TradeNexus becomes mission-critical global infrastructure.

## Scenario C (2040): Autonomous Physical Logistics
- **Driver**: Autonomous electric container ships and automated robotic port terminals.
- **Our Strategic Adaptation**: Direct digital protocol integration with autonomous port operating systems, clearing vessels prior to berth docking.

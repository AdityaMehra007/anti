### Record ID: DEC-001
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Decision**: Formally anchor the corporate beachhead around Autonomous Cross-Border Trade & Regulatory Compliance for Indian Exporters (TradeNexus AI).
- **Alternatives Considered**:
  1. *Developer DevTool / Agent Harness*: Saturated market, high churn, low willingness to pay among early tech adopters.
  2. *Healthcare / Clinical AI Assistant*: Prohibitive regulatory friction (CDSCO, HIPAA, US FDA), extreme legal liability, slow sales cycles.

- **Reasoning**: Cross-border trade is a multi-trillion dollar sector undergoing immense regulatory disruption (EU CBAM, US Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act, revised Indian Foreign Trade Policy). Mid-market exporters have massive budgets, clear quantifiable pain (demurrage fees), and high willingness to pay.
- **Evidence**: Over 4,500 export-oriented businesses verified in Bengaluru alone; average export customs fine exceeds ₹2,00,000 per delayed container.
- **Confidence**: 92%
- **Expected Outcome**: Secure first paid pilot ($1,000 / ₹85k) within 30 days of MVP release.

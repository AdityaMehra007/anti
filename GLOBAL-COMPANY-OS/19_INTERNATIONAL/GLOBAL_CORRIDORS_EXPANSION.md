# TRADENEXUS AI — GLOBAL CORRIDORS & BILATERAL TRADE EXPANSION

**Operational Horizon**: 2026 - 2030  
**Coverage**: India to EU, USA, and UAE CEPA Corridors  

---

## 1. CORRIDOR 1: INDIA → EUROPEAN UNION (GERMANY / NETHERLANDS / FRANCE)
- **Primary Ports**: Nhava Sheva (JNPT) / Chennai → Rotterdam, Hamburg, Antwerp.
- **Core Regulatory Drivers**:
  - **EU CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)**: Regulation (EU) 2023/956. Mandatory full quarterly carbon emissions reporting.
  - **Union Customs Code (UCC)**: Strict EORI and TARIC 10-digit classification.
  - **German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG)**: Human rights and environmental tracking across Tier-1 suppliers.
- **TradeNexus Capabilities**:
  - Full automated XML declaration compliant with the EU CBAM Transitional Registry.
  - Direct conversion from Indian 8-digit ITC-HS to European 10-digit TARIC codes.
  - Verification of European Consignee EORI status.

---

## 2. CORRIDOR 2: INDIA → UNITED STATES (EAST & WEST COAST)
- **Primary Ports**: Mundra / JNPT → New York / New Jersey, Long Beach.
- **Core Regulatory Drivers**:
  - **US CBP ACE (Automated Commercial Environment)**: Mandatory electronic manifest and entry summaries (CBP Form 7501).
  - **HTS-US (Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States)**: 10-digit tariff code verification.
  - **Section 301 & Section 232 Tariff Audits**: Special steel and aluminum tariffs.
  - **UFLPA (Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act)**: Mandatory supply chain traceability and raw material origin certification.
- **TradeNexus Capabilities**:
  - Automated cross-mapping between Indian ITC-HS and 10-digit HTS-US.
  - Bill of Lading and Commercial Invoice weight reconciliation against CBP entry summary limits.
  - Tamper-proof Country-of-Origin raw material lineage certification.

---

## 3. CORRIDOR 3: INDIA → UAE (COMPREHENSIVE ECONOMIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT - CEPA)
- **Primary Ports**: JNPT / Nhava Sheva → Jebel Ali (Dubai), Port Khalifa (Abu Dhabi).
- **Core Regulatory Drivers**:
  - **India-UAE CEPA (2022)**: Zero-duty access for 90% of Indian exports subject to strict Rules of Origin (Value Addition >= 40%).
  - **Digital Dubai Customs Mirsal 2**: High-speed digital clearance system.
- **TradeNexus Capabilities**:
  - Automated Certificate of Origin (CoO) value-addition computation (Origin Criterion: CTH + VA 40%).
  - Direct generation of CEPA-compliant commercial dockets to claim preferential zero-duty tariffs.

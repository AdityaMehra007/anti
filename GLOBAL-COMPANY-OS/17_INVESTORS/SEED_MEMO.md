## 1. The Core Insight & Macro Catalyst
Global merchandise supply chains are undergoing the most severe regulatory fragmentation in 50 years:
1. **EU CBAM (Regulation (EU) 2023/956)**: Border carbon tax on imported iron, steel, aluminum, and chemicals into Europe. Missing installation emissions XML filings incur automatic container holds, €50/tonne fines, and EUR 450/day port demurrage.
2. **US Customs (CBP) Enforcement**: Escalated audits under Section 232 / 301 and UFLPA supply chain origin traceability for automotive forgings, fasteners, and precision castings.
3. **Indian Export Surge**: India’s merchandise exports reached $447 Billion in 2024 and are projected by DGFT to reach **$1.0 Trillion by 2030**.
4. **The Friction Point**: 90% of Indian mid-market exporters still rely on manual customs brokers (CHAs) who re-key PDF/Excel invoices into ICEGATE software by hand. Tariff mismatches, typo queries, and missing CBAM declarations cause over **$1.8 Billion annually in port demurrage and detention losses**.

## 2. The Product: Autonomous Customs Clearance Engine
TradeNexus AI is an intelligent regulatory infrastructure engine that audits export documentation before cargo leaves the factory gate:
- **Instant Pre-Clearance (< 45s)**: Ingests commercial invoices, packing lists, and shipping orders; validates all line items against WCO 2026 and DGFT ITC-HS statutory gazettes.
- **EU CBAM XML Compiler**: Generates authenticated, installation-linked greenhouse gas emissions declarations ready for overseas port entry.
- **ICEGATE RES Shipping Bill Flat-File Generator**: Automatically outputs pre-validated Indian Customs Remote EDI System files (`.sb`), eliminating broker re-keying errors.
- **Demurrage Indemnity Shield**: Backs every approved docket with a ₹5,00,000 guarantee, transferring regulatory risk off the exporter.

## 1. Executive Product Narrative
TradeNexus AI transforms cross-border trade operations for Indian manufacturers. We solve the severe documentation and regulatory friction caused by new international compliance mandates (such as EU CBAM and US CBP Forced Labor enforcement). Our AI platform audits export shipping dockets in real time, validating 8-digit HS codes, duty drawback entitlements, and carbon intensity certificates before cargo gates in at ports.

## 2. AWS Cloud Architecture Plan
- **Inference & Models**: Amazon Bedrock (Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Haiku integration) + AWS Textract for baseline OCR validation.
- **Compute**: AWS Fargate / ECS for zero-management containerized compliance auditors.
- **Storage**: Amazon S3 (Glacier vaults for immutable 7-year statutory customs document retention).
- **Database**: Amazon Aurora Serverless (PostgreSQL) for ACID-compliant ledger transactions.

## 2. Indian Government Grants & Seed Programs
1. **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)**: Up to ₹20 Lakhs grant for proof of concept and ₹50 Lakhs debt-convertible debentures via DPIIT-approved incubators (e.g. NSRCEL IIM-B, C-CAMP).
2. **Karnataka Elevate 100**: Up to ₹50 Lakhs non-dilutive grant from Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KITS) for Karnataka-registered startups.
3. **MeitY TIDE 2.0 (Technology Incubation and Development of Entrepreneurs)**: ₹4 Lakh to ₹7 Lakh EIR (Entrepreneur-in-Residence) grant for emerging tech.

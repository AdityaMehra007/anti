## 1. Company & Mission Description
TradeNexus AI is an autonomous, AI-native cross-border trade compliance and customs intelligence engine built in Bengaluru, India. We eliminate multi-thousand dollar cargo delays and customs demurrage penalties for mid-market export manufacturers shipping to North America and Europe. By deploying autonomous document parsing and deterministic Harmonized System (HS) tariff classification against official global trade databases, TradeNexus delivers pre-cleared, audit-certified export dockets in under 45 seconds at 88%+ gross margins.

## 2. Technical Architecture on Google Cloud
- **Vision & Document Extraction**: Leveraging **Gemini 1.5 Flash** for high-velocity, sub-second extraction of unstructured commercial export invoices, packing lists, and multi-lingual bills of lading.
- **Deep Domain Legal Reasoning**: Routing edge-case tariff classification and EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) emissions compliance through **Gemini 1.5 Pro**.
- **Backend Infrastructure**: Containerized micro-services deployed on **Google Cloud Run** and **GKE Autopilot** for elastic auto-scaling during export month-end surges.
- **Data & Vector Storage**: **Google Cloud Spanner** + **Vertex AI Vector Search** for real-time querying across millions of Harmonized Tariff Schedule line items and bilateral Free Trade Agreement (FTA) rules.

## 2. Active Experiment Ledger

### Experiment EXP-001: Cold Outreach Teardown vs. Generic Demo Pitch
- **Hypothesis**: Exporters who receive a pre-computed compliance gap teardown specific to their recent shipment (showing exact potential demurrage loss) will convert to demo calls at 4x the rate of a generic software pitch.
- **Test Group A (Control, 25 leads)**: Generic B2B email pitching "AI-powered customs compliance software".
- **Test Group B (Variant, 25 leads)**: Hyper-personalized audit teardown (e.g. `AUDIT_SUNDRAM_FASTENERS_LIMITED.html`) highlighting specific EU CBAM / HS-code risks.
- **Metric**: Reply rate & Meeting booking rate.
- **Outcome / Status**: ACTIVE. (Benchmark target: >12% reply rate on Variant B).
- **Decision Rule**: If Variant B > 2.5x Control, mandate automated pre-computed teardowns for all outbound campaigns.

### Experiment EXP-002: Prepaid Pilot vs. Free Trial with Contingency
- **Hypothesis**: Requiring a token commitment fee of ₹15,000 for a 30-day trial will produce higher customer engagement (dockets uploaded) and higher long-term retention than a 100% free trial.
- **Test Group A**: 100% Free 14-day trial (up to 25 dockets).
- **Test Group B**: ₹15,000 30-day trial with 100% money-back guarantee if zero demurrage risks or tariff errors are discovered.
- **Metric**: Activation rate (% of signups uploading >10 dockets) and Day-30 paid conversion.
- **Outcome / Status**: PLANNED FOR LAUNCH (Days 15–45).
- **Decision Rule**: Adopt the model that maximizes `Activated Customers × LTV`.

### Experiment EXP-003: Self-Serve Web Upload vs. Email-Based Audit Gateway
- **Hypothesis**: Traditional factory shipping clerks prefer emailing PDF invoices to an automated inbox (`audit@tradenexus.ai`) rather than logging into a web dashboard.
- **Test Group A**: Web dashboard drag-and-drop.
- **Test Group B**: Dedicated email address that auto-replies with the signed `TN-CERT` PDF docket in 60 seconds.
- **Metric**: Weekly docket submission volume per account.
- **Outcome / Status**: ACTIVE SPECIFICATION.
- **Decision Rule**: If Email Gateway volume > 2x Web Dashboard, prioritize email and WhatsApp bot interfaces.

## 2. Stage Breakdown & Threshold Triggers

### Stage 0: The Autonomous Beachhead (0 to 10 Exporter Accounts)
- **Team**: 1 Human Founder + Autonomous AI Agent Swarm (Engineering, Outreach, Market Intel).
- **Core Focus**: Achieve initial customer traction; close first ₹85,000 paid pilot; prove zero-error compliance engine.
- **Hiring Trigger**: Do NOT hire any full-time employee until MRR crosses ₹2,50,000 and customer demand exceeds founder capacity.

### Stage 1: The Core Technical Cell (10 to 50 Exporter Accounts)
- **Revenue Run-Rate**: ₹25L–₹1 Crore ARR.
- **Key Hires**:
  1. *Forward Deployed Trade Engineer (FDTE)*: Manages custom ERP connections (SAP, Tally, Zoho Books) and ICEGATE EDI flatfile integration.
  2. *Senior Full-Stack / Platform Engineer*: Hardens microservices architecture, ensures 99.9% uptime, and scales document parsing pipelines.
- **AI Leverage**: 80% of sales outreach, code reviews, and initial docket audits handled by autonomous agents.

### Stage 2: The Multi-Cluster Growth Engine (50 to 500 Accounts)
- **Revenue Run-Rate**: ₹1 Crore–₹10 Crore ARR.
- **Key Hires**:
  - Head of Enterprise Sales & Association Alliances (FIEO, EEPC).
  - Head of Trade Regulatory Compliance (ex-Customs / Senior CHA specialist).
  - 3 Technical Account Executives (Peenya, Pune, Chennai clusters).
  - 4 Customer Success & Integration Specialists.
- **Total Headcount**: ~15 human specialists managing an enterprise customer base that traditionally required 150+ clerks.

### Stage 3: Institutional Enterprise (500+ Accounts)
- **Revenue Run-Rate**: ₹10 Crore–₹100 Crore ARR.
- **Team**: 40 Core Team members managing pan-India and international trade corridors (Vietnam, Thailand, UAE, Germany).

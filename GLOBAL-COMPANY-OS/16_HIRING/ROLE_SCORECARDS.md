# TradeNexus AI: Role Scorecards & Hiring Criteria

## 1. Role Scorecard: Forward-Deployed Trade Engineer (Hire #1)

- **Mission**: Ensure seamless integration between customer ERP systems (Tally Prime, SAP, Oracle, Zoho Books) and the TradeNexus compliance engine, ensuring zero-latency docket validation.
- **Key Outcomes (First 90 Days)**:
  1. Build and verify production Tally Prime and Zoho Books export invoice XML ingestion connectors.
  2. Reduce new customer integration onboarding time from 3 days to <2 hours.
  3. Maintain 99.9% uptime on ICEGATE EDI flatfile generation pipelines.
- **Competencies**:
  - Python / FastAPI / AsyncIO proficiency.
  - Deep familiarity with XML, EDIFACT, JSON-RPC, and ERP schema structures.
  - Working knowledge of Indian export documentation (Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Bill, ICEGATE formats).
  - High empathy for non-technical manufacturing export managers.

## 2. Role Scorecard: Technical Account Executive — B2B Exports (Hire #2)

- **Mission**: Close ₹25,000–₹50,000/month recurring compliance software subscriptions across targeted Indian export clusters.
- **Key Outcomes (First 90 Days)**:
  1. Close 10 new paid B2B exporter contracts (Target: ₹3,50,000 new MRR).
  2. Convert 40% of inbound/outbound pilot accounts to annual contracts.
  3. Conduct 25 high-signal technical compliance demos per month.
- **Competencies**:
  - Proven track record selling B2B SaaS or logistics/trade tech to mid-market Indian enterprises.
  - Deep domain credibility: understands HS codes, demurrage, Letters of Credit, and foreign customs friction.
  - Metric-driven: rigorously logs pipeline stages and deal progression in CRM.

---

## 3. Role Scorecard: Senior Trade Regulatory Specialist (Hire #3)

- **Mission**: Guarantee 100% legal accuracy of TradeNexus classification rules against DGFT, CBIC, WCO, and foreign port regulations (EU CBAM / US CBP).
- **Key Outcomes (First 90 Days)**:
  1. Audit and certify the TradeNexus 8-digit ITC-HS database across Chapters 72, 73, 76, 84, 85, 87, and 29.
  2. Maintain a zero-regulatory-error record across all certified export dockets.
  3. Publish weekly regulatory amendment updates incorporated into the automated rules engine.
- **Competencies**:
  - Qualified Customs Broker (Rule 6 / Regulation 9 license) or 10+ years senior CHA operational leadership.
  - Comprehensive mastery of Customs Act 1962, Foreign Trade Policy (FTP 2023), and General Rules of Interpretation (GRI).

# COMPETITOR MAP & STRATEGIC DISPLACEMENT

| Competitor | Target Segment | Pricing Model | Core Weakness | TradeNexus Asymmetric Counter |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Flexport / Freightos** | Enterprise Global Shippers | Commission on freight + heavy SaaS | Tied to physical freight brokerage; ignores mid-market Indian exporters | Pure-play digital compliance software agnostic to freight forwarder |
| **Descartes Systems / Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE** | Fortune 500 Multinationals | $50,000–$250,000 annual contracts | Complex 6-month legacy implementations; unusable for nimble exporters | 10-minute self-serve onboarding; zero integration fee; ₹25,000/mo |
| **Traditional Customs House Agents (CHAs)** | Local SME Exporters | Per-shipment informal broker fee | 100% manual; frequent paperwork errors; zero automated traceability | 60-second turn-around; automated mathematical proof of compliance |
| **Generic Document AI (Rossum, AWS Textract)** | Horizontal enterprise | Usage-based API per page | No trade domain knowledge; cannot validate HS rules, FTAs, or tariffs | End-to-end trade domain intelligence; validates legal rules, not just text |

# ASSUMPTION & HYPOTHESIS TRACKER

Every strategic assumption is tracked as a testable hypothesis.

---

### ASM-001: Exporter Willingness to Pay
- **Assumption**: Mid-market Indian exporters will pay ₹25,000–₹50,000/month for an automated compliance and customs dossier verification system.
- **Classification**: HYPOTHESIS
- **Test**: Conduct 20 direct outreach conversations offering a 1-month paid pilot at ₹25,000 ($300).
- **Validation Metric**: ≥ 2 paid pilot commitments out of 20 qualified discussions.

### ASM-002: Regulatory Pain Severity
- **Assumption**: New EU CBAM and ESG reporting mandates cause severe document friction for exporters of metals, chemicals, and textiles.
- **Classification**: VERIFIED FACT (Source: DGFT Trade Circular 2024/2025; Federation of Indian Export Organisations Trade Briefing).

### ASM-003: Technical Feasibility of Extraction
- **Assumption**: Commercial shipping bills, packing lists, and commercial invoices in varying PDF/scanned formats can be parsed with >98% field accuracy using vision-language models and OCR.
- **Classification**: STRONG INFERENCE (Verified through preliminary VLM benchmarks on standard international trade templates).

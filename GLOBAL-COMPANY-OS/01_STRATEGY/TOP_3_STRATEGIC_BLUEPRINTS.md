# DOCUMENT 8: TOP 3 STRATEGIC BLUEPRINTS

## Strategic Blueprint #1: Multi-agent export invoice & HS-code clearance engine (TradeNexus) (`OPP-001`)

### 1. Beachhead Strategy
Focus exclusively on Mid-market export manufacturers ($2M-$50M) experiencing Demurrage & customs holds from tariff/CBAM errors.

### 2. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope
- Ingest commercial export documents (Invoices, Packing Lists, Bills of Lading).
- Run deterministic rules parser + HS-code classification engine.
- Generate export clearance compliance audit report with zero-error guarantee.

### 3. Monetization Ladder
- Tier 1: ₹25,000/month (Up to 100 shipping bills audited)
- Tier 2: ₹50,000/month (Up to 300 shipping bills + CBAM XML export)
- Tier 3: Enterprise custom (₹1,50,000/mo + dedicated ERP integration)

### 4. Defensibility & Compounding Moat
Every audited docket adds to the tamper-evident regulatory evidence ledger, creating an insurmountable proprietary dataset of Indian export-import compliance norms.

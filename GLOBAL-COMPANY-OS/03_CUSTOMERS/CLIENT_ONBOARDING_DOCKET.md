# TRADENEXUS AI: CLIENT SELF-SERVE ONBOARDING DOCKET

**Welcome to TradeNexus AI.** Follow this 3-step protocol to activate autonomous customs clearance for your upcoming export shipments within 15 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Corporate Profile & Export Authorization (Time: 3 Minutes)
Provide the following statutory identifiers for your export entity:
- **Company Name**: Exact name registered on DGFT portal.
- **10-Digit Importer Exporter Code (IEC)**: e.g. `0712345678`
- **GSTIN**: 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number.
- **Authorized Port(s) of Loading**: e.g., `INMAA1` (Chennai Port), `INKAT1` (Kattupalli), `INBLR4` (Bengaluru Air Cargo), `INNSA1` (Nhava Sheva).

---

## Step 2: Commercial Document Upload (Time: 5 Minutes)
For each upcoming shipment, transmit or upload draft copies of:
1. **Commercial Invoice** (PDF or Excel format containing item description, unit price, quantity, and declared HS code).
2. **Packing List** (Net weight, gross weight, number of packages, container number).
3. **Draft Bill of Lading (BoL) / Airway Bill (AWB)**.
4. *Destination Specific*: European buyer VAT/EORI number or US Importer of Record number.

---

## Step 3: Verification & Pre-Clearance Certificate (Time: 45 Seconds)
- TradeNexus processes all line items against WCO, DGFT, EU CBAM, and US CBP tariff databases.
- Receive your **Pre-Shipment Customs Compliance Dossier** with verified SHA-256 certificate seal.
- Hand the certified docket directly to your Customs House Agent (CHA) to file the official Shipping Bill on ICEGATE with zero risk of revision.\n

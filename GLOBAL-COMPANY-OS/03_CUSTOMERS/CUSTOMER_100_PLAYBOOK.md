## 1. Channel Partner Multipliers (1-to-Many Distribution)
Direct outbound cannot scale to 100 accounts with 1 founder without hiring armies of SDRs.
**The Asymmetric Strategy: Channel Partnerships**
1. **Customs House Agents (CHAs)**:
   - CHAs handle 50–200 exporter clients each. They suffer from paperwork backlogs.
   - Partner Offer: "White-labeled TradeNexus Portal for CHAs". The CHA offers our automated audit tool to their clients as an elite value-added service.
   - Economic Split: 75% TradeNexus / 25% CHA recurring commission.
2. **Export Promotion Councils & Trade Associations**:
   - FIEO (Federation of Indian Export Organisations), EEPC India, Chemexcil.
   - Deliver free monthly webinars: "Demystifying EU CBAM & Digital Customs Compliance in 2027".
   - Convert 15–20 webinar attendees per session into software trials.

# MONTHLY STRATEGIC REVIEW TEMPLATE

## Review Dimensions
1. **Financial Health**: Cash balance, net burn, runway months, revenue delta.
2. **Customer Validation**: Number of discovery calls, active pilots, feedback themes, churn.
3. **Product Velocity**: Features shipped, test coverage %, bug count, processing latency.
4. **Autonomous AI Leverage**: % of operational workflows automated without human intervention.
5. **The One Strategic Priority for Next Month**: [Defined based on the biggest identified bottleneck].

# DOCUMENT 10: MASTER EXECUTION BLUEPRINT

## Business Entity: **TradeNexus AI**
**Operating Beachhead**: Autonomous Cross-Border Trade & Regulatory Clearance Engine

### 1. Product Specification & Microservices Architecture
- **Intake Service**: Ingests PDF/TXT commercial invoices, packing lists, and purchase orders.
- **HS-Code Engine**: Deterministic classification across 8-digit tariff lines with confidence scoring.
- **Compliance Auditor**: Verifies invoice completeness, mandatory DGFT/CBP fields, CBAM carbon applicability, and calculates demurrage risk.
- **Dossier & EDI Generator**: Compiles export clearance certificates (TN-CERT) and ICEGATE-compliant XML/EDI dossiers.

### 2. Web Portal & Landing Page Wireframe
- **Hero**: "Eliminate $10,000+ Port Demurrage Penalties with 60-Second Customs Clearance Verification."
- **Live Demo Widget**: Instant drag-and-drop commercial invoice audit running client-side.
- **Proof Metrics**: 0 Container Holds, 100% CBAM Compliance, 45-Second Audit Time.

### 3. Customer Onboarding Journey
- **Step 1 (Day 1)**: Upload 5 historical sample shipping bills for free automated compliance gap teardown.
- **Step 2 (Day 2)**: Present report revealing hidden tariff misclassifications and demurrage exposure.
- **Step 3 (Day 3)**: Activate ₹25,000/mo pilot on first 50 live export containers.

### 4. 30-60-90 Day Execution Milestones
- **Days 1–30**: Dispatch 50 personalized compliance audits to Bangalore exporters; secure 1st paid pilot (₹25,000–₹85,000 MRR).

- **Days 61–90**: Launch CBAM carbon dossier add-on; scale to 15 accounts (₹4,50,000 MRR / ₹54L ARR); reach cash-flow positive scale.

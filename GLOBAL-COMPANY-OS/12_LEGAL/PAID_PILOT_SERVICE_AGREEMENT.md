### TERMS & CONDITIONS

#### 1. Scope of the Paid Pilot
- **Duration**: Exactly thirty (30) calendar days from the Effective Date.
- **Consignment Allotment**: Pre-clearance verification for up to **ten (10) live commercial export shipments** (air or ocean containers).
- **Core Deliverables**:
  1. Instant automated verification of commercial invoices, packing lists, and shipping bill dockets.
  2. 8-digit Harmonized System (HS) tariff classification audit citing WCO/DGFT legal schedules.
  3. Pre-shipment Regulatory Clearance Certificate with cryptographic SHA-256 seal.
  4. European Union CBAM carbon emissions XML reporting dossier (where applicable).

#### 2. Commercial Consideration & Terms of Payment
- **Pilot Setup & Licensing Fee**: **₹25,000 (Twenty-Five Thousand Indian Rupees)** + applicable GST.
- **Payment Structure**: 100% advance upon contract signing via electronic wire transfer (NEFT/RTGS/UPI).

#### 3. 100% Performance Guarantee & Demurrage Indemnity
- In the event that any export consignment verified, pre-cleared, and certified with a **"TradeNexus Approved"** seal faces customs detention, demurrage, or port inspection penalties **solely attributable to a documentation error verified by TradeNexus**, Service Provider shall:
  1. Immediately refund 100% of the Pilot Fee (₹25,000); and
  2. Reimburse direct verified port detention penalties up to a maximum cap of **₹1,00,000 (One Lakh Rupees)** per verified consignment.

#### 4. Data Protection, Privacy & Non-Disclosure
- Client commercial invoices, buyer names, unit prices, and trade volumes are deemed strictly **Confidential Business Data**.
- Service Provider warrants that Client commercial data shall be encrypted using **AES-256 at rest** and **TLS 1.3 in transit**, and shall never be exposed to public artificial intelligence models or third parties without explicit authorization, in full compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023**.

#### 5. Transition to Annual Retainer
- Upon successful conclusion of the 30-day pilot with zero documentation penalties, Client shall have the exclusive right to roll the ₹25,000 pilot fee into an **Annual Enterprise Retainer (₹35,000/month)** covering unlimited monthly export shipments.

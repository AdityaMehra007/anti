## 2. Objective Conversion Triggers
1. **Trigger to Incorporate Private Limited in Bengaluru**:
   - Receipt of first paid pilot commitment (₹25,000–₹85,000).
   - Necessity of opening a corporate bank account with zero-rated export GST (LUT) authorization.
2. **Trigger for Delaware / Singapore Flip**:
   - Institutional cross-border Series A funding (> $2M) where US/global VCs mandate a US parent entity.
   - Cross-border enterprise SaaS contracts exceeding $100k/year where US corporate buyers mandate US jurisdiction.

## 3. Sovereign Indian Startup Advantages
- **DPIIT Startup Recognition**: Exempts entity from Section 56(2)(viib) (Angel Tax scrutiny) and provides access to fast-tracked patent examination (80% rebate on filing fees).
- **Section 80-IAC Tax Holiday**: 100% tax exemption on profits for 3 consecutive financial years out of the first 10 years.
- **Export LUT (Letter of Undertaking)**: Allows zero-rated export of software and compliance services outside India without upfront payment of IGST.

### Execution Roadmap for Incorporation in Bengaluru, Karnataka:
1. **Name Reservation**: File SPICe+ Part A with MCA (`VECTIS TRADE TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED` or similar).
2. **Incorporation**: SPICe+ Part B (incorporates Company, PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax, and Bank Account with HDFC/ICICI in one single filing).
3. **Registered Office**: Commercial coworking address in Indiranagar / Koramangala / HSR Layout, Bengaluru.
4. **DPIIT Recognition**: Apply on the Startup India portal immediately post-incorporation (takes 48–72 hours; unlocks fast-tracked patent examination and Section 80-IAC eligibility).
5. **Tax Exemptions**:
   - **Section 80-IAC**: 100% income tax deduction for 3 consecutive financial years out of the first 10 years (requires Inter-Ministerial Board approval, turnover $< ₹100$ Cr, incorporated before March 31, 2030).
   - **GST Letter of Undertaking (LUT)**: File Form GST RFD-11 annually to export software services at **0% GST** without payment of IGST.
6. **Non-Dilutive Government Grants (Karnataka)**:
   - **KITS ELEVATE 2027 (Idea2PoC)**: Apply for up to **₹50 Lakhs** equity-free grant under the Karnataka Startup Policy 2025–30.
   - **"Government First" Scheme**: Apply for **₹25 Lakhs** state government pilot work orders to validate trade export documentation for state-run export corporations (MSIL, KSIC).

## Phase 24: The 2035–2050 Adaptive Civilization Horizon

We evaluate the company against 6 civilizational scenarios:
1. **Scenario A (Universal Autonomous AI Agents)**: Trade documents are generated and consumed purely machine-to-machine. `VECTIS` acts as the cryptographic verification arbiter verifying that machine-generated shipping manifests match physical IoT sensor data.
2. **Scenario B (Autonomous Physical Supply Chains & Robotics)**: Autonomous cargo ships, drone deliveries, and robotic port cranes require real-time legal liability handoffs. `VECTIS` provides the immutable event ledger.
3. **Scenario C (Geopolitical De-Globalization & High Tariffs)**: Bounded trade blocs with conflicting sanctions and tariffs make automated real-time compliance indispensable.
4. **Scenario D (Green Energy & Carbon Economy)**: Every unit of global physical trade requires lifecycle carbon footprint tracking. `VECTIS` embeds carbon verification into every trade invoice.

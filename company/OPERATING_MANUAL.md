## 1. SALES CLOSING & DEAL INTAKE SOP (CRO DEPARTMENT)

### Step 1: The First 3 Minutes of Discovery
- Always begin by setting the agenda and removing sales pressure.
- Clarify that if the prospect doesn't match our capacity, we will decline the engagement.

### Step 2: Diagnostic Discovery Checklist
- [ ] What is their current Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)?
- [ ] How many outbound reps or ad dollars are they burning each month?
- [ ] How many sales calls does their team need every month to hit growth goals?
- [ ] If an extra 15 qualified calls land on their calendar, how many can they realistically close?

### Step 3: Presenting the Guarantee & Collecting Payment
- State the price clearly: \$3,000 setup fee + \$1,500/month recurring.
- Introduce the Risk Reversal: 15 Qualified Meetings in 45 days or 100% refund.
- Collect payment immediately via Stripe checkout link before leaving the Zoom call.

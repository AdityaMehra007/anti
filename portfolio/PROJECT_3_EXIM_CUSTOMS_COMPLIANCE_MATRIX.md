## 1. Executive Summary
Cross-border freight delays, port demurrage, and customs classification disputes cost global enterprises millions annually. This portfolio framework presents an operational audit protocol designed to streamline import/export shipments through Indian customs ports (Nhava Sheva JNPT, Chennai Port, Kempegowda International Airport Air Cargo Complex Bengaluru).

It systematically resolves:
1. **Risk Transition Ambiguity**: Precise mapping of liability handovers under **Incoterms 2020**.
2. **Documentary Discrepancies under UCP 600**: Elimination of non-compliant Letter of Credit (LC) documentation.
3. **Customs Valuation & Duty Optimization**: Accurate Harmonized System (HS) code classification and Basic Customs Duty (BCD) + Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS) + Integrated GST (IGST) mathematical reconciliation.

## 2. Incoterms 2020 Risk & Cost Transition Blueprint

| Incoterm 2020 | Risk Transfer Point | Freight Cost Obligation | Marine Insurance Mandate | Export Customs Clearance | Import Customs Clearance | Recommended Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EXW** (Ex Works) | Seller's factory/warehouse | Buyer | Buyer | Buyer | Buyer | Domestic / Multimodal |
| **FCA** (Free Carrier) | Handover to named carrier | Buyer | Buyer | **Seller** | Buyer | Any / Containerized |
| **FOB** (Free On Board) | On board vessel at load port | Buyer | Buyer | **Seller** | Buyer | Sea / Inland Waterway |
| **CIF** (Cost, Insurance & Freight)| On board vessel at load port | **Seller** (to dest port) | **Seller** (LMA/IUA Clause C)| **Seller** | Buyer | Ocean Cargo Only |
| **DDP** (Delivered Duty Paid)| Delivery at buyer destination | **Seller** | Seller | **Seller** | **Seller (including duties)**| Multimodal / Courier |

> **Operational Insight for Bengaluru GCCs**:
> For tech components imported into Bengaluru Air Cargo (BLR), **FCA Bengaluru** or **CIP Bengaluru** is superior to FOB/CIF because risk transfers upon handover to the primary air carrier, preventing maritime maritime insurance disputes for inland multimodal transport.

## 4. Duty Calculation Model (Sample 8-Digit HS Code)

- **Product**: Industrial Electronic Sensors & Connectors
- **HS Code**: `8536.90.90`
- **Assessable Value (CIF)**: ₹10,00,000.00 INR

| Tax Component | Statutory Rate | Base Calculation | Payable Amount (INR) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Customs Duty (BCD)** | 7.5% | ₹10,00,000 × 7.5% | ₹75,000.00 |
| **Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS)** | 10.0% of BCD | ₹75,000 × 10.0% | ₹7,500.00 |
| **Subtotal (Landed Value)** | — | Assessable + BCD + SWS | ₹10,82,500.00 |
| **Integrated GST (IGST)** | 18.0% | ₹10,82,500 × 18.0% | ₹1,94,850.00 |
| **Total Customs Duty Payable** | — | BCD + SWS + IGST | **₹2,77,350.00** |

*Note: IGST paid is fully creditable as Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Indian GST law.*

## 5. UCP 600 Letter of Credit Compliance Checklist

Before presenting shipping documents to the negotiating bank, verify:
- [x] **Commercial Invoice**: Signed, in original, currency matches LC, no charges exceeding LC value.
- [x] **Bill of Lading / Air Waybill**: Marked "Clean on Board", consigned "To Order of Issuing Bank", freight status matches Incoterm ("Freight Prepaid" or "Freight Collect").
- [x] **Insurance Policy**: Dated on or before date of transport document, covering 110% of CIF value, in same currency as LC.
- [x] **Packing List**: Exact gross and net weights matching transport documents; no discrepancy in package count.
- [x] **Certificate of Origin**: Stamped by authorized chamber of commerce, claiming applicable FTA preferential tariff concession (e.g. CECA, AIFTA).

---

### Phase 2: The 14-Day Sprint — SQL for Business & Operations Analytics (PostgreSQL / SQLite)
* **Goal**: Master relational querying to pass technical screening assessments for Business Analyst roles.
* **Daily Breakdown**:
  - **Days 8-9**: Database fundamentals, DDL/DML, primary/foreign keys, schema design for supply chain operations.
  - **Days 10-11**: Aggregations and grouping (`GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, multi-column aggregates, date-time functions).
  - **Days 12-13**: Complex multi-table joins (`INNER`, `LEFT`, `FULL OUTER`, self-joins for hierarchical category trees).
  - **Days 14-16**: Window Functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`, `RANK()`, `DENSE_RANK()`, `LEAD()`, `LAG()`, running totals, moving averages).
  - **Days 17-19**: Common Table Expressions (CTEs), Subqueries, and temporary reporting tables.
  - **Days 20-21**: Capstone Project: Analyze a 5,000-order international shipment database (on-time delivery rate, customs delay hotspots, port dwell time analysis).
* **Target Deliverable**: *Cross-Border Supply Chain Performance SQL Query Pack (GitHub Repository)*.

---

### Phase 3: The 30-Day Sprint — Enterprise Business Intelligence (Power BI / DAX)
* **Goal**: Build an interactive web-hosted business intelligence dashboard to demonstrate to hiring managers.
* **Weekly Breakdown**:
  - **Week 1 (Days 22-28)**: Data modeling in Power BI (Star schema vs Snowflake schema, relationships, cardinality).
  - **Week 2 (Days 29-35)**: DAX Fundamentals (Calculated Columns vs Measures, `CALCULATE`, `FILTER`, `ALL`, Time Intelligence).
  - **Week 3 (Days 36-42)**: UI/UX & Information Architecture for Executives (visual hierarchy, bookmark navigation, drill-through pages).
  - **Week 4 (Days 43-49)**: Publishing to Power BI Service, automated dataset refreshes, embedding in web portfolio.
* **Target Deliverable**: *Live Interactive Bangalore IT Corridor & Logistics Intelligence Dashboard*.

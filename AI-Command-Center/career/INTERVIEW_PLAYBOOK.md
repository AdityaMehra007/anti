### Q2_CONFLICT_VENDOR: How do you handle a vendor or supplier who fails to deliver as per SLA or attempts price gouging?
**Category:** `Vendor & Supply Chain Management`

- **Situation (S):** While delivering a 1,200-attendee corporate summit in Bengaluru, a key fabrication and audiovisual vendor attempted to levy unapproved 25% rush surcharges and delayed staging handover.
- **Task (T):** Enforce contractual rate card integrity, prevent unbudgeted cost overruns, and ensure the staging was fully commissioned 2 hours before attendee entry.
- **Action (A):** I benchmarked their itemized rate card against pre-agreed master SLAs, isolated the unsubstantiated line-item markups, and negotiated an immediate performance cure window with vendor leadership while deploying standby local backup technicians.

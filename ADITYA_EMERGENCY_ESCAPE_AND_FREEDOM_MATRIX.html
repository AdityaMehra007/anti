ZERO FEAR ARMOR

Your Statutory Rights Under RBI Directives

Never let any bank or collection agency intimidate you or your family. The Reserve Bank of India has established strict criminal and regulatory penalties for unlawful recovery practices.

  • RBI Master Circular (DOR.MCS.REC.28/01.01.001/2022-23): Recovery agents are legally strictly prohibited from calling before 8:00 AM or after 7:00 PM.
  • Zero Harassment Law: Agents CANNOT use abusive language, make false threats, visit your workplace without prior appointment, or contact your relatives/mother.
  • The Banking Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in): If any agent violates these norms, filing a 1-click complaint with RBI results in severe regulatory fines on the bank and immediate cessation of calls.
  • Police Protection: Under Section 503 & 506 of IPC (Criminal Intimidation), aggressive threats by recovery agents are criminal offenses.

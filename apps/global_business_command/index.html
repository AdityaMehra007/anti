Zero-Tax Export Framework (GST LUT & RBI e-FIRC)

How to legally export IT & consulting services from India to the world with 0% tax friction.

1. Form GST RFD-11 (Letter of Undertaking / LUT):
Under Section 16 of the IGST Act, the export of services is classified as a "Zero-Rated Supply". By filing a free annual LUT on the GST portal, you bill US, UK, EU, and UAE clients with 0% IGST without paying tax upfront or claiming refunds.

2. Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (e-FIRC):
Whenever funds arrive from Wise or Stripe, the Authorized Dealer bank issues an electronic FIRC confirming receipt of convertible foreign exchange. This guarantees full compliance with RBI FEMA regulations.

3. Section 44ADA Presumptive Taxation:

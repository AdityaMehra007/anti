## 1. THE LUXURY FASHION MAISONS & LEATHER GOODS

Bangalore’s luxury fashion operates through **UB City (The Collection)**, standalone heritage flagship villas on Lavelle Road, and high-end luxury conglomerate divisions (Reliance Brands Limited, Aditya Birla Fashion Luxury).

### Top Houses & Flagships
1. **Louis Vuitton (The Collection, UB City)**
   * *Domain:* Haute maroquinerie (luxury leather goods), trunk-making, ready-to-wear, fragrances.
   * *Vibe:* The undisputed anchor of Indian luxury retail. Elite client list spanning South Indian cinema icons, industrial heiresses, and tech founders.
2. **Gucci (The Collection, UB City)**
   * *Domain:* High-fashion leather goods, ready-to-wear, fine footwear.
   * *Vibe:* Dynamic, fashion-forward, heavily frequented by young multi-millionaire creators and entrepreneurs.
3. **Burberry (The Collection, UB City)**
   * *Domain:* British heritage outerwear, signature trench coats, luxury accessories.
4. **Reliance Brands Limited (RBL Luxury Portfolio - Bangalore Retail)**
   * *Houses Represented:* **Balenciaga, Bottega Veneta, Giorgio Armani, Salvatore Ferragamo, Canali, Jimmy Choo, Tiffany & Co. (curated pop-ups), Michael Kors.**
   * *Corporate Office:* Vittal Mallya Road / UB City.
5. **Aditya Birla Fashion & Retail Luxury (The Collective)**
   * *Address:* 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar & UB City.
   * *Portfolio:* Multi-brand luxury retail housing **Ralph Lauren, Ted Baker, Fred Perry, Hackett London, Karl Lagerfeld.**

### Key Luxury Roles & Earnings
* **High-End Client Development Director / VIC (Very Important Client) Manager:**
  * *What you do:* You don't stand on a sales floor. You curate private salon viewings inside private VIP suites. You fly to Paris/Milan for Fashion Week with billionaire clients, send bespoke floral gifts on birthdays, and manage personal wardrobing.
  * *Earnings:* ₹18L – ₹35L Base + High VIC Commission = **₹35L – ₹65L+ / year**.
* **Store Director / Boutique General Manager (Louis Vuitton / Gucci):**
  * *What you do:* Full P&L responsibility for an ₹80Cr–₹200Cr luxury boutique. Managing high-touch clientele, security protocols, and international supply allocation.
  * *Earnings:* **₹30L – ₹55L / year** + annual luxury performance bonuses.
* **Luxury Visual Merchandiser & Scenographer (RBL / ABFRL Luxury):**
  * *What you do:* Designing high-fashion window displays, editorial runway setups, and launch galas.
  * *Earnings:* ₹12L – ₹24L / year.

## 2. HAUTE HORLOGERIE (LUXURY TIMEPIECES) & HIGH JEWELRY

The market where single items trade for **₹15 Lakhs to ₹3 Crore+**. The highest density of wealth concentration per square foot in Bangalore.

### Boutiques & Authorized Salons
1. **Ethos Summit (The Collection, UB City)**
   * *Flagship Brands:* **Rolex, Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Jaeger-LeCoultre, IWC Schaffhausen, Panerai, Breitling, Hublot, Zenith.**
   * *Aura:* Private viewing vaults with champagne service for collectors seeking allocated references.
2. **Art of Time & Rodeo Drive Luxury (Central Business District)**
   * *Specialty:* Independent haute horlogerie (Cartier, Chopard, Ulysse Nardin, Bovet) and vintage timepieces.
3. **Tanishq / Zoya (Titan Company Luxury Division)**
   * *Flagship:* Zoya Boutique (Vittal Mallya Road, Opp. UB City).
   * *Domain:* Bespoke diamond jewelry, rare gemstones, high-jewelry collections priced up to ₹10 Crore.
4. **C. Krishniah Chetty Group of Jewellers (CKC 1869)**
   * *Heritage Flagship:* The Touchstone, Main Guard Cross Road.
   * *Aura:* Royal jeweler to the Mysore Maharajas and British nobility. Old-money diamond royalty.

### Key Roles & Earnings
* **Haute Horlogerie Private Client Specialist (Watch Allocator):**
  * *What you do:* You manage the confidential allocation of waitlisted references (Rolex Daytona, Patek Philippe Nautilus 5711, Audemars Piguet Royal Oak). You host private cigar and single-malt tasting nights for private collectors.
  * *Earnings:* ₹15L – ₹28L Base + Allocation Commission = **₹40L – ₹80L+ / year**.
* **Zoya / Heritage High-Jewelry Private Client Advisor:**
  * *What you do:* Personal diamond consultations for bridal dynasties, royal families, and industrialist matriarchs.
  * *Earnings:* ₹14L – ₹26L Base + Jewelry Ticket Cut = **₹35L – ₹70L / year**.
